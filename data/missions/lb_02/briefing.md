CHALK BOARD // KETTLE FLAT // SUMMER 2001
FROM: JESSUP CALLOWAY-ROSS, WAR-CAPTAIN  TO: COMMANDER OF THE ROAD RIGS

THE SURVEY CONVOY IS ASH AND THE AUTHORITY HAS NOT TAKEN THE HINT. IT HAS STARTED PUTTING POSTS ON THE OLD ROADS: A TURRET, A FEW RIFLES, A FLAG.

THREE OF THEM SIT BETWEEN THE DRIFT AND THE RAIL LINE. ONE AT THE EAST END OF THE NORTH ROAD. ONE ON THE WEST ROAD. ONE AT THE SOUTH BEND. WHILE THEY STAND, NO CARAVAN CROSSES THE BELT.

THE MOTHER RIG IS DOWN AND HAS A MUSTER RIG AND A YARD. RAISE A GARAGE RIG AND A LOOKOUT; THE LOOKOUT IS THE GATE THAT LETS THE MUSTER RIG TEACH SLINGERS. SLINGERS BREAK TURRETS, RIFLES DO NOT. EACH TURRET DRAWS ITS POWER FROM A PLANT SET WELL BACK FROM THE GUN. KILL THE PLANT FIRST AND THE TURRET IS A WALL. TAKE THE POSTS ONE AT A TIME.

THEY WILL SEND A COLUMN WHEN THE FIRST POST GOES SILENT. BE HOME WHEN IT ARRIVES.

THE SPEAKER ASKS THAT THE POST CREWS BE LEFT THEIR BOOTS. THEY WILL NOT BE LEFT MUCH ELSE.

SOMEBODY SAID THE AUTHORITY HID A STASH IN THE NORTH CORNER OF THE BELT. LOOK IF YOU HAVE A DAY.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
