OFFICE OF RETURN  //  SECOND RELIEF FLEET  //  TELEX 0518  //  22 APRIL 2001
TO: COMMANDER, LANDING FORCE
FROM: COMMISSIONER FOR THE RETURN
RE: THE SUPPLY LINE

1. THE FIRST BEACHHEAD HOLDS. THE HYDROGRAPHIC OFFICE NOW ASKS FOR A SECOND LANDING ON THE NORTH SHORE, WHERE THE WATER IS QUIET ENOUGH FOR A DOCK.

2. A PARTY IS ALREADY ASHORE WITH THE DEPOT, POWER AND A BILLET. WE WOULD BE GRATEFUL IF YOU WOULD RAISE THE ANCHORAGE ON THE SHALLOWS. ONCE IT STANDS, THE HOME SHIPMENTS CAN FIND US.

3. DR. ROQUE ASKS THAT NOBODY BUILD ANYTHING THAT CANNOT BE PUT RIGHT BY A WRENCH. HE ALSO ASKS ABOUT THE HULL NUMBERS. I HAVE TOLD HIM THEY ARE FINE.

4. THE AUTHORITY KEEPS A GARRISON ON THE COAST ROAD, A DAY'S MARCH TO THE EAST. IT WILL COME FOR THE DOCK. WE ASK THAT THE ROAD BE CLEARED.

5. EVERY CONVOY COSTS THE CONCORDAT A MONTH. PLEASE LET NONE BE WASTED.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
