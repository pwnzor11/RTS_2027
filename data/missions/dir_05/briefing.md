INTERIOR BROADCAST  //  SHAFT FOUR  //  2001  //  PRIORITY: AFTER DARK
TO: SHIFT FOREMAN, NIGHT SHIFT
FROM: THE VOICE, FOR THE DIRECTOR

1. CITIZENS IN SERVICE: THE HOUR IS LATE. THE CHILDREN OF THE INTERIOR ARE ASLEEP. YOU ARE NOT. A LULLABY, THEN A SHIFT LIST.

2. THE TENANCY KEEPS A LINE OF SEARCHLIGHTS ALONG THE ROAD NORTH OF THE PIT. THREE POWER PLANTS FEED IT. A LIGHT THAT CANNOT SEE IS A LIGHT THAT CANNOT BE BLAMED.

3. YOUR SHIFT: EIGHT CONSCRIPTS AND TWO SAPPERS. NO SHAFT. NO RESERVE. THE HATCH WILL CLOSE BEHIND YOU. CUT ALL THREE PLANTS. THE SAPPERS CARRY THE CHARGES. THE CONSCRIPTS CARRY THE SAPPERS.

4. BE HOME AT THE HATCH BEFORE DAWN. THE HATCH OPENS FOR NO ONE AFTER DAWN. A WISE FOREMAN BRINGS EVERY SAPPER HOME.

5. SLEEP WELL, CITIZENS. WE WILL BE AWAKE.

-- END OF BROADCAST --
