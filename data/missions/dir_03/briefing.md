INTERIOR BROADCAST  //  GLASS COAST  //  2001  //  PRIORITY: THE PADS
TO: COMMANDER, FORWARD SHIFT
FROM: THE VOICE, FOR THE DIRECTOR

1. CITIZENS IN SERVICE: THE ROADS ARE CUT. THE SHIFT NOW STANDS ON THE GLASS COAST, WHERE THE GREAT DRIFT OF 1990 UNCOVERED THE OLD LAUNCH SITES. THE DIRECTOR HELD THEM IN TRUST. THEY ARE TO BE RECLAIMED.

2. TWO SITES, TWO FRONTS. PAD SEVEN LIES IN THE CENTRE OF THE COAST. PAD NINE LIES TO THE NORTH-EAST. EACH IS WATCHED BY A SEARCHLIGHT MAST AND TWO RIFLEMEN. REMOVE THE MAST, THEN STAND A CITIZEN ON THE GROUND UNTIL THE SITE IS SECURE.

3. THE TENANCY WILL SEND RELIEF COLUMNS FROM THE SOUTH AND THE EAST. THE FIRST IS EXPECTED SOON. BREAK BOTH. A SITE IS NOT HELD UNTIL ITS RELIEF IS DEAD.

4. YOUR SHIFT NOW HAS THE ISOTOPE WORKS AND THE SMELTER VENT. THE ASHTHROWER IS A GOOD WEAPON, AND IT BURNS EVERYONE STANDING IN ITS ASH. DO NOT KEEP YOUR CITIZENS IN IT.

5. A TENANCY WATCH POST SITS ON THE RIDGE TO THE SOUTH. NOT REQUIRED. THE SEA WIND IS COLD TONIGHT. COME HOME.
