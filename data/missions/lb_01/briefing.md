YOU HAVE A MOTHER RIG, A HANDFUL OF HANDS AND A MUSTER BOARD. UNPACK ON CLEAR GROUND SOUTH OF THE ROAD. RAISE POWER AND A MUSTER RIG. WHEN THE CONVOY LEAVES THE DEPOT, TAKE IT AT THE CROSSING.

THE SCRAPERS ARE THE PRIZE. THE SLINGERS ARE YOUR ONLY ANSWER TO THEIR ARMOUR. DEAL WITH ANY RELIEF THEY CALL.

THE SPEAKER WANTS IT KNOWN THAT THE CREWS HAVE FAMILIES. SO DO WE. THE LEDGER IS LONG AND THE ROAD IS SHORT.

NOBODY IS COMING TO HELP US. THAT IS WHY WE ARE COMING.
