INTERIOR BROADCAST  //  ASH RECKONING  //  2001  //  PRIORITY: ORDER SEVEN
TO: COMMANDER, FORWARD SHIFT
FROM: THE COMMISSARIAT, READ BY THE VOICE

1. CITIZENS IN SERVICE: THE PADS ARE HELD. ORDER SEVEN, WRITTEN BY THE DIRECTOR IN 1963, IS NOW IN FORCE. IF THE SURFACE REFUSES TO SUBMIT, THE WARHEADS UNDER THE LAUNCH SITES ARE TO BE DETONATED. THE COMMISSARIAT HAS PREPARED THE SUNRISE.

2. THE SUNRISE DEVICE LIES IN ITS CHAMBER BENEATH YOUR KEEP. IT NEEDS THREE THOUSAND TICKS TO CHARGE AND CONTINUOUS POWER. KEEP THE REACTOR VENTS STANDING. IF THE DEVICE IS DESTROYED, THE ORDER FAILS.

3. THE OLD TUNNEL HEAD RUNS SOUTH BENEATH THE RIVER. ITS ENTRANCE IS THE HATCH ON THE SOUTH BANK. THE DEVICE MAY FIRE ONLY WHERE A HATCH STANDS WITHIN EIGHT CELLS OF THE TARGET. THE TARGET IS THE TENANCY'S ASH RIDGE COMMAND. HOLD THE HATCH.

4. THE TENANCY WILL SEND ITS WHOLE STRENGTH AT THE HATCH. IT HAS BUILT A BASE TO THE SOUTH. CITIZENS DO NOT FARE WELL AGAINST ITS RIFLEMEN. USE NUMBERS. DO NOT STAND CITIZENS IN YOUR OWN ASH.

5. WHEN THE DEVICE IS CHARGED, FIRE IT. ONE SHOT IS ENOUGH. A TENANCY POST SITS ON THE WEST BANK. NOT REQUIRED. THE WEATHER TONIGHT IS CLEAR, COMMANDER. THE STARS ARE OUT. I AM NOT SUPPOSED TO SAY SO.

-- END OF BROADCAST --
