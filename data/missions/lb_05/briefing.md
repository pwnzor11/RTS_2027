CHALK BOARD // KETTLE FLAT // SPRING 2001
FROM: JESSUP CALLOWAY-ROSS, WAR-CAPTAIN  TO: COMMANDER OF THE FORWARD RIGS

NOT EVERY BAND ANSWERED THE GATHERING. THE RIG NOBODY'S DAUGHTER AND TWO OTHERS, TWO FIRES AND LONG HAUL, HOLD A WELL AT THE WEST END OF THE BELT AND FLY THEIR OWN FLAGS. THEY HAVE FOUGHT US FOR THAT WATER SINCE BEFORE THERE WAS A BANNER. THEY WILL NOT SHARE IT WITH A BANNER NOW.

THE MOTHER RIG IS DOWN WITH POWER, A YARD AND A MUSTER RIG. THE COLUMN WITH YOU IS ALL THE SLINGERS WE HAVE. THERE IS NO LOOKOUT YET AND NO MORE WILL BE MADE.

TWO FIRES AND LONG HAUL SIT ON THE NORTH-WEST ROAD: A DYNAMO RIG, A MUSTER RIG, A FEW NESTS. BREAK THEM FIRST. LEAVE THEM STANDING AND THEY WALK TO THE WELL AND STAND WITH NOBODY'S DAUGHTER. WHEN THE ROAD FALLS THE WELL WILL SEND ITS ANSWER AT THE CAMP. BE HOME FOR IT.

THEN THE WELL. DYNAMO RIG, MUSTER RIG. THE GATHERING MOVES ON AT THE DEADLINE AND I WILL NOT LEAD IT AWAY WITH A WELL STILL FLYING A BAD FLAG.

THE SPEAKER ASKS THAT THEIR NAMES STAY ON THE FLAG. THE SPEAKER ALSO SENDS A TINKER AND A REQUEST: THE RIG THAT CARRIES THOSE NAMES SHOULD COME BACK WHOLE, IF IT CAN. I ASKED HER WHAT A RIG IS WORTH IF IT WILL NOT BURN. SHE SAID, IT CAN BE TAKEN.

ONE FLAG. THAT IS THE WHOLE ARGUMENT.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
