CHALK BOARD // GLASS COAST // AUTUMN 2001
FROM: JESSUP CALLOWAY-ROSS, WAR-CAPTAIN  TO: COMMANDER OF THE DUNE RIGS

THE DRIFT IS ACROSS THE ASH AND ON THE GLASS COAST. THERE IS A CAMP AT LAST, WITH A GARAGE AND A LOOKOUT. AND IN THE MIDDLE OF THE DUNES, A HOLE.

THE DIRECTORATE HAS BROKEN THE SURFACE HERE: THREE HATCHES, A REACTOR VENT, A LOUDSPEAKER MAST AND A FEW PILLBOXES. EVERY HATCH PUTS SOLDIERS ON OUR DUNES. THE PILLBOXES REACH THREE CELLS. THE NEW HARPOON WAGONS REACH FIVE. THE VENT FEEDS THE PILLBOXES. DO THE ARITHMETIC.

THE AUTHORITY KNOWS WE ARE HERE. A COLUMN IS COMING DOWN FROM THE NORTH TO THE CAMP WITH JEEPS AND A TANK. LEAVE HANDS AT HOME. YOU DO NOT GET BOTH WARS FOR FREE.

THE SPEAKER ASKS ONLY THAT WE DO NOT BE THE LAST ONES STANDING IN A HOLE. SHE HAS BEEN IN A HOLE.

IF YOU CAN, TAKE THE MAST DOWN. THE VOICE HAS BEEN TELLING US TO REGISTER FOR SERVICE FOR TWO YEARS.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
