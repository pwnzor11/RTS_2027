CRA FIELD COMMAND  //  GLASS COAST SECTOR  //  PRIORITY URGENT
TO: CONVOY COMMANDER
RE: COAST ROAD, OPERATION LOW TIDE

1. THE JUNCTION HOLDS AND THE GRAIN MOVES. THE NEXT TASK IS THE COAST. CHIEF SURVEYOR FENN'S PARTIES MUST REACH THE SHORE BEFORE THE WINDOW CLOSES.

2. TWO CARRIERS, THREE TANKS, TWO JEEPS, FOUR RIFLES. A FORWARD YARD STANDS AT THE START POINT. YOU HAVE ITS FACTORY, ITS BARRACKS AND ITS CASH.

3. THE DIRECTORATE HAS A PICKET ON THE BEACH AND RAIDERS ON THE ROAD. THEY WILL ALSO GO FOR THE YARD WHEN THE CONVOY LEAVES. DO NOT LEAVE IT EMPTY.

4. THE DUNES ARE NOT EMPTY. WE HAVE SEEN CARAVAN FIRES. THEY ARE NOT FRIENDS OF OURS, AND WE ARE NOT FRIENDS OF THEIRS. THAT IS A LONG STORY.

5. THE CARRIERS ARE THE MISSION. LOSE THEM BOTH AND WE LOSE THE COAST.
