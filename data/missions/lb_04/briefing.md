CHALK BOARD // THE ASH PLAINS // WINTER 2001
FROM: JESSUP CALLOWAY-ROSS, WARLORD OF THE DRIFT  TO: COMMANDER OF THE BANNER RIGS

THEY CAME BY SEA. A FLEET OF STRANGERS WITH CLEAN SHIPS AND MANIFESTS HAS PUT A DEPOT ON OUR SOUTH SHORE AND CALLS IT A BEACHHEAD. THE AUTHORITY SENT A LETTER. THE DIRECTORATE SENT A VOICE. THESE SENT GUNS.

WE ARE THE LAST ONES ON THIS GROUND. THE SPEAKER HAS GIVEN ME THE BANNER FOR THE WINTER. AFTER THIS NOBODY ASKS ANYONE FIRST AGAIN.

THEIR TWO GUN BATTERIES REACH EIGHT CELLS AND SEE EVERYTHING ON THE NORTH FACE. THEY RUN OFF TWO PILE HOUSES AT THE EAST END OF THE BEACH. COME FROM THE EAST. DARKEN THE HOUSES. THEN BREAK THE DEPOT.

THEY WILL LAND A COUNTER-FORCE ON THE PLAINS BEFORE YOU MOVE. BREAK IT AT THE CAMP. THEN THE FLEET HAS A SECOND WAVE ON THE WATER AND NO PATIENCE: IF THE DEPOT STANDS WHEN IT ARRIVES, THE COAST IS THEIRS.

THE ROTOR PAD AND THE SECOND DRIFT ARE YOURS TO BUILD. THE DRIFT BURIES A BEACH FOR FORTY SECONDS. IT DOES NOT KILL. USE IT ON THE ONES WHO SURVIVE.

THERE IS A WRECK OF THEIR SUPPLY LANDER OUT ON THE EAST PLAINS. WHOEVER REACHES IT FIRST KEEPS THE CRATES.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
