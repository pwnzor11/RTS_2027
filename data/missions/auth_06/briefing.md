CRA FIELD COMMAND  //  RUST BELT SECTOR  //  PRIORITY URGENT
TO: BASE COMMANDER
RE: MILL TOWN, OPERATION RAIL LINE

1. THE DIRECTORATE HAS DUG IN UNDER THE RUINED MILL TOWN ON THE RAIL LINE. FOUR HATCHES AND TWO REACTOR VENTS HAVE BEEN COUNTED FROM THE EMBANKMENT. A SHIFT LIVES UNDER THEM. WHILE THEY HOLD THE TOWN, NOTHING MOVES ON THE RAIL.

2. THIS IS A BASE, NOT A COLUMN. A YARD, POWER, A REFINERY AND A BARRACKS ARE STANDING IN THE SOUTH-WEST CORNER. THE FACTORY IS YOURS TO RAISE. THE CLEARANCE FOR TANKS, SCRAPERS, SILOS, RATION HALLS AND THE SEISMOGRAPH IS LIFTED FOR THIS OPERATION. USE ALL OF IT.

3. THERE IS PITCHBLENDE ON A SPUR WEST OF THE TOWN. TWO SCRAPERS ON IT WILL PAY FOR THE REST. WATCH THE RATION COUNT: A ROAD OF TANKS NEEDS A RATION HALL.

4. THE GRAIN TRAIN LEAVES WHEN THE CLOCK RUNS OUT. IF THE TOWN IS NOT OPEN IT TURNS BACK, AND THE ADMINISTRATOR WILL HAVE TO EXPLAIN WHY TO THE COUNTY.

5. THE OLD GRAIN ELEVATOR STANDS ON THE NORTH-EAST SIDE OF THE TOWN. THE ADMINISTRATOR ASKS THAT IT STAY STANDING. I ASK THAT YOU AIM CAREFULLY.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
