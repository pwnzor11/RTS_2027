OFFICE OF RETURN  //  SECOND RELIEF FLEET  //  TELEX 0471  //  9 APRIL 2001
TO: COMMANDER, LANDING FORCE
FROM: COMMISSIONER FOR THE RETURN
RE: DREDGE RIGHTS

1. THE SURVEY PARTY HAS THE CHARTS OF THE SOUTH COAST. THE ASH LIES THICK ON THE SHALLOWS OFF THE BEACH, AND NOBODY ELSE HAS A BOAT TO TAKE IT. WE WOULD BE GRATEFUL IF THE FLEET WOULD LAND THERE.

2. THE FLEET MUST PAY FOR ITSELF. PLEASE PUT THE COMMAND SHIP ASHORE ON THE BEACH, RAISE A DREDGE WORKS AND PUT TWO SKIMMERS ON THE ASH. EVERY LOAD IS A LOAD THE CONCORDAT DOES NOT HAVE TO SEND.

3. THE AUTHORITY KEEPS A COAST BATTERY ON THE GROUND EAST OF THE BEACH. IT HAS RANGED THE SANDBAR. IT WILL RANGE THE SHALLOWS NEXT. WE ASK THAT IT BE BROKEN.

4. DR. ROQUE WILL SEND DRAWINGS FOR A HOVER YARD AS SOON AS THE DREDGE STANDS, AND TWO GLIDE TANKS UNDER THEIR OWN POWER. HE ASKS THAT THEY BE TREATED AS PROTOTYPES. THEY ARE THE ONLY ONES WE HAVE.

5. THE WRECK ON THE SANDBAR WAS A SHIP OF THE FIRST RELIEF FLEET. HE WOULD LIKE ANYTHING THAT CAN BE TAKEN FROM HER, IF THE GUNS ALLOW IT.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
