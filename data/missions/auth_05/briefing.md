CRA FIELD COMMAND  //  RUST BELT SECTOR  //  PRIORITY URGENT
TO: COLUMN COMMANDER
RE: HATCH COUNTRY, OPERATION LISTENING POST

1. THE SURVEY CONVOY HELD. THE RAIDERS THAT HIT IT CAME FROM NOWHERE. THERE IS NO NOWHERE. THERE IS A HOLE IN THE GROUND AND A DOOR OVER IT.

2. YOUR COLUMN: TWO JEEPS, SIX RIFLEMEN, TWO ROCKETEERS, TWO COMBAT ENGINEERS. NO YARD. NO FACTORY. WHAT YOU LOSE STAYS LOST.

3. NORTH OF THE PIT THE GROUND IS FULL OF SURFACE HATCHES. THREE OF THEM FEED THE ROAD. CLOSE THEM. AN ENGINEER CAN TAKE A HATCH; A ROCKET WILL SHUT ONE FOR GOOD.

4. A LOUDSPEAKER STANDS ON A POLE AMONG THE HATCHES. IT READS OUT EVERY COLUMN THAT PASSES, BY NAME, TO EVERYONE UNDERGROUND. PULL IT DOWN BEFORE IT FINISHES YOURS.

5. KEEP TO THE DRY GROUND. THE ASH IS DEEP AROUND THE PIT AND WILL KEEP ANY JEEP THAT STRAYS.
