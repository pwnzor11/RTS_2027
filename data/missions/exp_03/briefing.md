OFFICE OF RETURN  //  SECOND RELIEF FLEET  //  TELEX 0731  //  9 JULY 2001
TO: COMMANDER, LANDING FORCE
FROM: COMMISSIONER FOR THE RETURN
RE: THE FLEET REACHES THE COAST

1. THE FLEET IS OFF THE WEST COAST. THE ADMIRAL ASKS FOR A COVE WHERE THE CARRIER CAN LIE UP AND HER AIRCRAFT CAN BE KEPT IN THE AIR. WE WOULD BE GRATEFUL IF YOU WOULD HOLD ONE.

2. AN ADVANCE PARTY IS ASHORE WITH THE DEPOT AND THE HOVER YARD. PLEASE RAISE A FLIGHT DECK AND A SAM MOUNT BEFORE THE CARRIER ARRIVES. HER AIRCRAFT CARRY FIVE ROUNDS EACH AND THEY MUST COME HOME TO REARM.

3. TWO POWERS HAVE COME INTO THE DUNES. THE AUTHORITY IS COMING DOWN THE NORTH ROAD. THE DIRECTORATE IS SURFACING IN THE SOUTH. THEY ARE NOT FRIENDS, BUT THEY ARE BOTH LOOKING AT US.

4. THE ADMIRAL ASKS THAT NO DESTROYER BE LOST. HE REMARKS THAT HE HAS NEVER LOST A CONVOY AND WOULD LIKE TO KEEP IT SO.

5. RETURN WITH THE TIDE.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
