INTERIOR BROADCAST  //  SHAFT FOUR  //  2001  //  PRIORITY: FIRST LIGHT
TO: COMMANDER, FORWARD SHIFT
FROM: THE VOICE, FOR THE DIRECTOR

1. CITIZENS IN SERVICE: THE INTERIOR HAS RETURNED. SHAFT FOUR HAS BROKEN THE SURFACE ON THE OLD RAIL BED. THE TENANCY CALLS IT AN INTRUSION. WE CALL IT A PAYMENT RECEIVED.

2. YOUR SHIFT HOLDS ONE SHAFT, FOUR CITIZENS AND A BUDGET. RAISE A REACTOR VENT FOR POWER. FROM THE MUSTER HATCH, CALL FORWARD CONSCRIPTS.

3. OPEN A HATCH ON THE MARKED GROUND. THE HATCH IS THE FIRST DOOR THE TENANCY WILL SEE. MAKE IT HOLD.

4. FOUR CITIZENS ARE TO STAND AT THE HATCH WHEN IT OPENS. THE TENANCY WILL SEND A RESPONSE. EXPECT IT IN TWO WAVES.

5. A TENANCY PICKET SITS TO THE NORTH-EAST. IT HAS EYES ON YOUR SHAFT. A WISE COMMANDER WOULD CLOSE THEM. THE WEATHER ABOVE IS GRIM. COME HOME.

-- END OF BROADCAST --
