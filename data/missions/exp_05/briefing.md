OFFICE OF RETURN  //  SECOND RELIEF FLEET  //  TELEX 0427  //  28 MARCH 2001
TO: COMMANDER, SURVEY PARTY
FROM: COMMISSIONER FOR THE RETURN
RE: THE SOUTH COAST

1. THE FIRST RELIEF FLEET OF 1997 WAS NEVER HEARD FROM AGAIN. THE SURVEY HOVER HAS NOW FOUND A HULL AGROUND ON A SANDBAR OFF THE SOUTH SHORE, BY THE OLD RAILHEAD AT TERN POINT. IT MAY BE ONE OF THEIRS.

2. THE BEACH IS EMPTY OF ALL BUT US. THE WRECK IS NOT. WE UNDERSTAND SCAVENGERS ARE WORKING IT FOR METAL. YOUR PARTY: ONE SURVEY HOVER, EIGHT MARINES, TWO PATHFINDERS. THERE IS NO DEPOT AND NO SHIPMENT. WHAT YOU LOSE STAYS LOST.

3. WE WOULD BE GRATEFUL IF THE MARINES WOULD CLEAR THE WRECK AND THE HOVER WOULD TAKE ITS SIGNAL LOG ABOARD. THE LOG GOES TO THE ADMIRAL AS IT IS. HE ASKS THAT NOBODY READ IT BEFORE HIM.

4. A LAUNCH WILL WAIT IN OPEN WATER SOUTH OF THE SHALLOWS UNTIL THE TIDE TURNS. IT WILL NOT WAIT LONGER.

5. THE OLD EVACUATION PLATFORM STANDS ON THE POINT. IF A MARINE CAN SPARE THE WALK, THE HYDROGRAPHIC OFFICE WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHAT IS WRITTEN ON IT.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
