# Drill: Hold the line

**Authority. Practice range.**

Three waves will come through the east gate. You have five riflemen, one tank, one turret and a power plant. You also have 2400 credits, a Construction Yard and a Barracks: you can build Turrets and train Riflemen. Waves get heavier, so do not sit still.

Medals go by how many units and structures you lose. The limits are shown when you finish.
