CRA FIELD COMMAND  //  RECKONING SECTOR  //  PRIORITY URGENT
TO: ARMY COMMANDER
RE: SUNRISE DEVICE, OPERATION LAST LIGHT

1. THE DIRECTORATE IS PREPARING A DEVICE OF THE OLD EXCHANGE. THEY CALL IT THE SUNRISE. WE CALL IT A SECOND EXCHANGE IN THE MAKING. THE ORDER THEY WORK FROM IS SEVEN.

2. THE DEVICE STANDS IN THEIR BASE ON THE SOUTH BANK OF THE RIVER. THE COUNTDOWN IS ON THE TIMER AND NOBODY HAS A WAY TO STOP IT BUT YOU.

3. EVERYTHING WE HAVE IS AT YOUR YARD: ARMOUR, BULWARKS, TWO HOWITZERS WHOSE RANGE IS LONGER THAN THEIR NESTS, AND ALL THE CASH IN THE TREASURY. SPEND IT.

4. THEY WILL RAID THE YARD WHILE YOU CROSS. LEAVE A GUARD. LIEUTENANT DRAY HAS CROSSED TO OUR SIDE AND WILL SPEAK ON THE RADIO. LISTEN TO HIM.

5. THE ADMINISTRATOR ASKS THAT NOTHING BE CALLED A VICTORY. NOTED. THE DEVICE IS TO BE DESTROYED.

-- END OF MESSAGE --
