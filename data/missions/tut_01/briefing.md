# Basic Training

**Authority. Training ground, northern range.**

Every Commander starts here. This is a short, safe drill: nothing on this range can end your career, and there is no clock.
An Instructor will talk you through each lesson over the radio and mark your current objective.

You will learn to:

1. Select soldiers and move them.
2. Attack a target.
3. Build a Power Plant, then a Barracks.
4. Train infantry.
5. Collect pitchblende with a Refinery and a Scraper.
6. Defeat a small enemy camp.

You begin with a Construction Yard and four riflemen. The range is small: your base sits in the north-west corner, the practice
target is due east, and the enemy camp is in the far north-east. Take your time, and watch the objective list on the right.
