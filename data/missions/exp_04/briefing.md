3. THE LAST SHIPMENTS ARE AT SEA. DR. ROQUE REPORTS THAT NOT EVERY HULL WILL SEE HOME. HE ASKED ME TO SAY THAT HE IS SORRY ABOUT THAT, AND THAT I SHOULD NOT PASS IT ON. I HAVE PASSED IT ON.

4. THERE ARE PAPERS IN MY DESK. I HAVE NOT READ THEM. I WOULD PREFER NOT TO HAVE TO.

5. RETURN WITH THE TIDE.
