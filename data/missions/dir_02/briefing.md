2. THREE POSTS WATCH THE CONVOY ROADS: NORTH, WEST AND EAST. EACH IS A SEARCHLIGHT MAST WITH A SMALL GARRISON. REMOVE ALL THREE.

3. TWO CONVOYS RUN BETWEEN THE POSTS. THE FIRST MOVES SOON. THE SECOND FOLLOWS ON THE TIMER. WHAT THEY CARRY WILL NOT REACH THE TENANCY.

4. YOUR SHIFT NOW HAS A SMELTER VENT AND AN ASSEMBLY BORE. THE TENANCY RESPONDS IN SMALL NUMBERS. CITIZENS DO NOT FARE WELL AGAINST A RIFLEMAN IN OPEN GROUND. USE NUMBERS, USE THE ASHTHROWER, USE THE SAPPER ON THE MAST.

5. A ROAD DEPOT SITS TO THE SOUTH. NOT REQUIRED. NOT FORBIDDEN. THE WEATHER IS DRY TODAY, COMMANDER. DRESS WARMLY.
