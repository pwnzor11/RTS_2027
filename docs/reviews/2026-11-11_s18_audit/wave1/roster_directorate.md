## Booklet caveats (from the faction-ad audit, /home/claude/audit/faction-ad/findings.md)
- The sidebar shows the Cameo name, not the archive title (e.g. dir_bunker reads "Bunker" in game for a wall piece) (FAD-15).
- dir_agitprop: aura_rof_permille = 1250 is read by the sim as a 25 % slower reload for conscripts, ashthrowers and sappers in 4 cells, not a buff (FAD-02). It has no speed aura.
- dir_commissar has no aura or other mechanic; it is a 400-credit pistol infantry (FAD-09).
- dir_ash_sower and dir_dirigible lay radiation / Blackout only where they fire at an enemy actor; there is no attack-ground (FAD-05).
- irradiated = true (volunteer, crawler) has no effect today: no warhead sets bonus_vs_irradiated_percent (FAD-18).
- Directorate structures share the Authority's health, cost and power numbers one for one (FAD-20).
- Campaign: informant, assessor, volunteer, bore, agitprop, ash sower, delver, cistern, bunk gallery, bunker wall and redoubt are never buildable; the dirigible is in the dir_04 allow-list but cannot be built because aircraft are always refused in restricted missions (FAD-03, FAD-04). dir_bore_head is reachable by upgrading a Hatch (the morph does not check the allow-list).
- Tunnels: tunnel_size S = 1 point, M = 3 points; network cap 10 points in transit, transit 100 ticks (game.toml [tunnels]); entrances draw units in at ticks_per_point per point. L units (crawler, land-train) never use the network.
