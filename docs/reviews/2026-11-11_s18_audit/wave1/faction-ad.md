# faction-ad: Authority and Directorate rules data, item by item — findings

**Scope read:**
- Every `auth_*` / `dir_*` entry in data/rules (infantry, vehicles, buildings, upgrades, techs, weapons, warheads, game, terrain).
- The code that reads them: UnitRulesLoader, UpgradeRulesLoader, StructureRulesLoader defaults, TierSugar, RulesDeriver, and the Simulation Combat, Production, Upgrades, Superweapon, Effects, Ground, Submerge, Burrow, Supply and Players files, plus AirMission.
- AI: the Authority and Directorate RecruitFaction files, TierPlanner (superweapon, air and anti-air parts), data/ai/difficulty.toml, FactionAiKeys.
- Campaign gating: the two campaign files, the eight auth/dir mission.toml files, CampaignService, MissionHost.
- In-game names and tooltips: CameoNames, CameoTooltip, UpgradeEffectText.
- Text: data/archive (A/D entries), docs/lore units, structures and upgrades.
- Design 02 §2, 03, 05, 08 §3-4, 13, 14; both campaign docs; balance docs m3_balance, m4_16_rules, s9_tables, s15_siege; DECISIONS D-028, D-054, D-132, D-152, D-164..D-201.

## Summary (worst first)
1. **The Authority superweapon can never fire** (FAD-01). The AI already knows this and never builds it; a human pays 3 000 for nothing.
2. **The Agitprop slows its own infantry** instead of boosting it (FAD-02).
3. **No aircraft can be built in campaign missions** (FAD-03).
4. **The campaign allow-lists leave out core units.** The Authority has no Rocketeer and no replacement Scrapers; the Directorate cannot build Bunk Galleries, so its supply cap is permanent (FAD-04).
5. **Support vehicles cannot target ground** (FAD-05), and their zero-damage sprays still deal 1 damage per hit (FAD-13).
6. **Splash ignores the air/ground split.** Flak hurts its own base; ground splash hurts aircraft overhead (FAD-06).
7. **Tier-3 placeholders.** The Bulwark is a copy of the Tank, the Howitzer is weaker than the tier-2 Seismic Mortar, the aircraft are slower than infantry, and the Crawler is dominated by the Land-Train (FAD-07, FAD-12).
8. **The range ladder inverts design 03.** Tier-1 defences are outranged by enemy tier-1 units, and the tier-1 Rocket Truck beats the Tank (FAD-08).
9. **Missing identity mechanics.** No ashfall hazard, no Commissar aura, `irradiated` does nothing, Directorate structures are copies, and 25 types have no art (FAD-09, -10, -11, -18, -20).

## Findings

### FAD-01 · critical · DATA-REF · confidence high
**What:** `auth_atomic_battery` can never fire.

**Where:** data/rules/buildings.toml:570 (`superweapon_entrance_range = 8  # ASSUMED (M4-07)`); src/Sim/Simulation.Superweapon.cs:181, 185-190; Simulation.Tunnels.cs:416; src/AI/Recruit/TierPlanner.cs:392-401.

**Evidence:** `HasEntranceInRange` needs an own building whose `entrance` is not none. No `auth_*` building sets `entrance`, so `ApplySpecial` and the UI click (GameScreen.cs:789) always refuse the shot. The AI's `SuperweaponUsable` returns false in exactly this case, so the AI never builds the battery. D-132(4) fixed the identical setting on `lb_second_drift` (8 → 0), and `exp_salvo_array` already ships with 0 (buildings.toml:891, 1225).

**Impact:** A 3 000-credit tier-3 structure that charges to "ready" and never fires. It is the auth_04 reward (mission.toml:20, kept by D-164). It only works if the player captures a Directorate Hatch.

**Fix idea:** Set the range to 0. Add a data test that a faction with a ranged superweapon owns an entrance building. Size S.

### FAD-02 · major · NUMBERS · confidence high
**What:** `dir_agitprop` slows the reload of nearby conscripts, ashthrowers and sappers by 25 %.

**Where:** vehicles.toml:130 (`aura_rof_permille = 1250`); Simulation.Effects.cs:19-22 and 152-155; Simulation.Combat.cs:247.

**Evidence:**
- The sim multiplies reload time by the aura value. It takes the lowest value below 1000 as a buff and treats values above 1000 as debuffs.
- `dir_rifle` reload goes from 16 to 20 ticks, so conscript DPS drops from 7.5 to 6.0.
- Lore: "steadies and hurries nearby conscripts". Design 03: "boost nearby conscripts".
- There is no `aura_speed_permille`, so "hurries" does nothing.
- The only test (DirectorateDataTests.cs:317-319) checks radius and jam, not the direction of the aura.

**Fix idea:** Set 800 (and optionally speed 1100). Add a test that buff auras are below 1000. Needs D-152 approval. Size S.

### FAD-03 · major · CODE · confidence high
**What:** In every campaign mission with an allow-list, aircraft are always locked.

**Where:** src/Scripting/MissionHost.cs:302-315 (`IsTypeAllowed` maps only Infantry, Vehicle, Structure and Defence to an id; Aircraft falls to `_ => null`, which returns false); Simulation.Production.cs:106.

**Evidence:**
- `dir_dirigible` and `dir_mooring` are unlocked by dir_03 and listed in dir_04's `starts_with`, but the dirigible cannot be queued.
- The Authority gets `auth_airfield` from auth_03 for nothing.
- exp_03/04 list `exp_jet`, `exp_gunship` and `exp_skyhook`, and lb_03 lists `lb_rotor_pad`; they are hit the same way.
- No test queues an aircraft in a mission.

**Fix idea:** Add an Aircraft case that returns the vehicle id, plus a Scripting test. Note that upgrades are never filtered (`CanResearch` does not call `UnlockFilter`). Size S.

### FAD-04 · major · DATA-REF · confidence high
**What:** The campaign tech climb never grants several core items.

**Where:** the `[starts_with]` / `[unlocks_on_win]` tables of auth_01-04 and dir_01-04; CampaignService.cs:81-90 (a strict allow-list); no mission script grants tech.

**Evidence:**
- Authority, never buildable:
  - `auth_rocketeer`, its only anti-armour infantry.
  - `auth_scraper`: a lost harvester can only be replaced by building a second 2 000-credit Refinery.
  - `auth_silo`.
  - `auth_seismograph`, even though its archive unlock is `campaign:auth_01`.
  - `auth_blockhouse`, `auth_seismic_mortar`, `auth_foam_truck` and both aircraft.
- Directorate, never buildable: `dir_bunk_gallery`, `dir_cistern`, `dir_bunker`, `dir_redoubt`, `dir_informant`, `dir_assessor`, `dir_volunteer`, `dir_bore`, `dir_agitprop`, `dir_ash_sower`, `dir_delver`.
- Supply is on in missions. The Directorate cap is 20 in dir_01 (shaft only) and 40 in dir_02-04 (two pre-placed galleries), and it can never be raised or rebuilt. The Authority can build Ration Halls from auth_02.

**Impact:** The Authority campaign has no anti-armour infantry and no new harvesters. A dead gallery permanently shrinks the Directorate army.

**Fix idea:**
- Add rocketeer, scraper and silo to auth_01/02.
- Add the seismograph to auth_01's unlocks.
- Add bunk gallery and cistern to dir_01.
- Add a test that every tier-1 type is reachable by the last mission.

Size S.

### FAD-05 · major · DESIGN · confidence high
**What:** Decon Truck, Foam Layer, Ash Sower and Dirigible apply their ground effect only by attacking an enemy or neutral actor in range (1.5 cells; 2 for the Dirigible). There is no attack-ground order.

**Where:** Simulation.Combat.cs:139-141 and 404-414; Players.cs:60-64; Command.cs:8; ground effects are applied only in `Detonate` (Combat.cs:338-339).

**Evidence:**
- The Decon Truck cannot clean contaminated ground with no enemy on it.
- The Foam Layer cannot pre-seal ground against surfacing (D-077). Burrowed and surfacing units are untargetable.
- The Ash Sower (250 HP) must close to 1.5 cells of an enemy.
- The M4-16 foam test laid foam directly in the harness, so this never showed up.
- The AI builds Foam trucks and Ash Sowers (AuthorityRecruitFaction.cs:27, DirectorateRecruitFaction.cs:42).

**Fix idea:** Add an AttackCell command, or automatic behaviours for these trucks. Owner decision. Size M.

### FAD-06 · major · SIM · confidence high
**What:** `Detonate` splash hits every actor within the radius, air or ground.

**Where:** Combat.cs:340-388 (filters only Garrisoned, Submerged and Burrowed); weapons.toml:181 (`auth_flak_gun` uses warhead `auth_he`); warheads.toml:18 (`auth_he` has splash 1 and hits allies).

**Evidence:**
- Flak killing a Dirigible over an own Power Plant: 13 shells, each dealing 14 to the plant (7 if the plant is adjacent to the impact cell). That is about 180 of the plant's 400 HP per kill.
- Ground splash weapons such as the Rad Mortar and Ashthrower damage aircraft overhead.
- Bombers in a stack take half damage from each other's `auth_he` bombs.
- Review B-1 (2026-11-03) predicted exactly this kind of per-kind omission.

**Fix idea:** Skip actors whose air/ground status differs from the primary target's, unless the weapon targets both. Needs a hash re-pin. Size S.

### FAD-07 · major · NUMBERS · confidence high
**What:** Several tier-3 types are placeholder copies. This is still open from the s9_tables owner questions; no D-number answers it.

**Where:** vehicles.toml:275-333 (the comment at :251 says so), :370-384; weapons.toml:165, 199.

**Evidence:**
- **Bulwark (T3)** is identical to the Tank (T1): 800 credits, 400 HP, speed 20, same cannon. Lore: "slow … bank vault".
- **Howitzer (T3, 800)** is dominated by the Seismic Mortar (T2, 800): 20 damage against 28 with the same rate of fire and versus row, and the mortar adds burrow hits, 2x against entrances and 50 % garrison pass-through. The howitzer only has range 10 against 9. Its DPS against structures is 3.1.
- **Interceptor and Bomber** share 600 cost, 200 HP and speed 8 = 0.62 cells/s, slower than a Rifleman (0.94). Air flies at type speed (AirMission.cs:51). Lore: "fast", "most expensive aircraft"; design 03: "best air".
- **Great Dirigible** uses the Dirigible's chassis and has lost the Blackout that design 08 gives it.

**Fix idea:** Give the owner a numbers proposal and measure. Needs D-152 approval. Size S + M.

### FAD-08 · major · NUMBERS · confidence medium
**What:** Both sides' tier-1 defences are outranged by enemy tier-1 units, and the Directorate has no tier-1 anti-vehicle defence. Design 03 says the opposite: Directorate "poor long-range firepower", Authority "strong defence".

**Evidence (ranges in cells):**
- Tank (5 cells, 12 DPS against fortified) kills a Pillbox (3 cells) in about 32 s with no return fire. A Rocketeer does it in about 34 s, and the Jeep (3.5) also outranges it.
- Rocket Nest is air-only; the first Directorate anti-vehicle defence is the tier-2 Rad Mortar.
- Rocket Truck (T1, 8 cells, targets both) kills a Turret (5 cells) in about 33 s with no return fire, and is faster than the Tank (1.72 against 1.56 cells/s).
- m3_balance.md:41: "4 rocket trucks beat 4 tanks at 90 salvo damage". The proposed cut to 60 (line 38) was not applied.
- s15_siege.md:21 noted the ranges and the AI was taught to exploit them (D-174); nobody raised them as a balance question.

**Fix idea:** Owner choice. Options: pillbox range 4-5 or a Directorate tier-1 anti-vehicle emplacement; turret range 6; Rocket Truck to 6 cells or 60 damage / 900 cost. Size S-M.

### FAD-09 · major · DESIGN · confidence high
**What:** The Commissar (infantry.toml:81-94) has no aura or other mechanic. Lore: "conscripts fight harder near one".

**Evidence:** It costs 400 for 90 HP and 5 DPS. Five Conscripts cost the same and give 250 HP and 37.5 DPS. The AI buys it (DirectorateRecruitFaction.cs:41).

**Fix idea:** Move a buff aura to it (e.g. radius 3, `aura_rof_permille = 850`), or make it cheap. Size S.

### FAD-10 · major · DESIGN · confidence high
**What:** The ashfall hazard is not implemented, although D-028 (confirmed) and design 02 §2 require it.

**Evidence:**
- `RunRadiation` (Ground.cs:67-95) reads only weapon-laid radiation.
- Radiation is written only by warheads, superweapons and storms.
- So `rad_resist_permille`, the "ashfall-proof" Carrier, Lead Lining and the "ashfall barely bothers" conscripts only matter against weapon radiation.

**Fix idea:** Implement it (e.g. ash density ≥ N counts as level K in the damage pass) or record a decision that drops it. Size M.
