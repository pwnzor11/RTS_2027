# faction-le: The Left Behind and the Overseas Expedition — findings
Scope read: every lb_/exp_ entry in the rules TOMLs; the loaders (Unit, Structure, Rules, TierSugar, the attribute lists in EffectRulesLoader, SkirmishStart). Sim partials: Rigs, Salvage, Raid, Storm, Naval, Landing, Beachhead, Shipments, Submerge, Supply, Superweapon, NoRebuild, Veterancy, Effects (auras), Combat (Fire/Detonate/ComputeDamage), Production (lanes, factories, defeat), BurrowMove (deploy), Ground (radiation, hidden units), and the SalvageMission, AirMission and CombatMissions files. Outside the sim: the MissionHost unlock filter, ExpeditionSetup, the sidebar's IsHidden, ShipyardReach. AI: both RecruitFaction files, data/ai and FactionAiKeys. Docs: design 06, 07 and 10, DECISIONS D-087..D-201, the archive TOMLs (they are the tooltip source), the lore id check, m6_10_ai, m7_08_expedition_data, s9_tables. Campaigns: both campaign files and the 8 mission.toml files.

## Summary (worst first)
1. In campaign missions no aircraft can ever be built (MissionHost.IsTypeAllowed rejects the Aircraft category).
2. An Expedition player that never placed a building is never defeated, so the game cannot end.
3. The Mother Rig keeps building structures while packed; the D-102 pause never fires on real data.
4. The placeholder costs leak into veterancy, wrecks and the sidebar: instant Hero, 1000-credit wrecks, and a 6000-credit tender that does nothing.
5. Submarines: a dived sub has no counter for Authority, Directorate or Left Behind. A surfaced sub is near-immune to the Expedition.
6. Non-Expedition warheads were padded to 100 % against ship armour, so ships are effectively unarmoured.
7. The Left Behind campaign is capped at 20 supply and never gives the Hauler.
8. The Tow does nothing, the Minelayer does nothing, and many tooltips promise mechanics that don't exist.

### FACTION-LE-01 · critical · CODE · high
**What:** a player with a tech list can never build aircraft, even ones the list grants.
**Where:**
- src/Scripting/MissionHost.cs:302-314 (`_ => null` returns false for ProductionCategory.Aircraft).
- Simulation.Production.cs:35-39: air types live only in the Aircraft category.
- Affected grants: lb_04/mission.toml:18,26 (lb_rotor, lb_kite), exp_03:19,30 and exp_04:17,28 (jet, gunship, skyhook).
**Test gap:** CampaignServiceTests checks only the Vehicle, Infantry and Structure categories.
**Impact:** the air arm is missing from the finale of both campaigns (and from other factions' missions too). Bot walks still pass because the bot builds no air.
**Fix:** add an Aircraft case to the switch, plus a test. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-02 · critical · SIM · high (code read, not run)
**What:** UpdateDefeat (Simulation.Production.cs:516-531) defeats only a player whose `EverHadBase` flag is set. Packed rigs count as a base; the Expedition Command Ship does not. UpdateNoRebuildDefeat (NoRebuild.cs:88) also skips players without the flag.
**Cases where the Expedition player is never defeated:**
- (a) it never deploys the Command Ship;
- (b) the Command Ship is sunk before it deploys; even after the Marines, Skimmer and Landing Craft die, the player stays undefeated;
- (c) SkirmishStart.SpawnUnits finds no water within ring 16 and silently skips the ship. The map validator checks only walkability, not distance.
**Impact:** an endless skirmish or AI match (the normal game has no tick limit, D-153 (7)). Possibly the cause of the unexplained Ash Plains Expedition stall (D-197).
**Fix:** treat a founder unit, or any start kit, as having had a base. Add a test: remove the Command Ship at tick 1 and expect defeat 2400 ticks later. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-03 · major · SIM · high
**What:** RigLanePaused (Rigs.cs:99-117) checks only buildings with `Produces == category`. Produces can never be Structure or Defence: StructureRulesLoader.cs:41 allows only "", infantry, vehicle and aircraft, and lb_mother_rig has `produces = ""`.
**Effect:**
- Up to 5 queued structures and 5 queued defences keep progressing while the Mother Rig packs and travels.
- AdvanceLane never re-checks prerequisites.
- ApplyPlace (Production.cs:191-203) accepts the finished building within 2 cells of any own building.
**Test gap:** RigTests.cs:22 sets `Produces = Structure` in its fixture, a value TOML cannot express.
**Impact:** relocation costs no build time.
**Fix:** pause these lanes while no live, unpacked conyard-root building exists, and fix the fixture. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-04 · major · NUMBERS · high
**What:** three placeholder costs leak into play: lb_mother_rig_packed 4000 (vehicles.toml:530), exp_supply_tender 6000 (:749), exp_command_ship 6000 (:859).
**Effects:**
- Kill value is the rules cost (Veterancy.cs:37-48) against thresholds 600/1600/3200, so one lethal hit on a tender (250 HP, unarmed, meant to be raided per D-126) or on any packed rig makes the shooter Hero.
- The wreck is min(1000, 25 % of cost) (Salvage.cs:98). A packed 200-credit Still Rig leaves a 1000 wreck; the unpacked one leaves 50.
- Every packed rig becomes one generic 1000 HP heavy vehicle at the same HP ratio. That triples the effective HP of 250-300 HP rigs (1200 with Scrap Plate).
- The tender shows in the sidebar, where only auth_conyard is hidden (SidebarPanel.cs:598). A tender the player builds never pays and costs 6000.
**Fix:** set real costs (packed rig = the carried rig's cost, tender 0), hide these types from building, or exempt them from kill value and wrecks. Size S-M.

### FACTION-LE-05 · major · DESIGN · high
**What:** a dived sub is hidden unless an own-team sonar covers it (Submerge.cs:23-62, M3.cs:192), and only Submerged-class weapons can target it.
**Who has the tools:** sonar exists only on exp_destroyer and exp_picket; the only submerged-class weapon is exp_launch_charge. A dived sub still fires (D-143).
**Numbers:** exp_sub_missile does 110 damage, 120 % vs structures, range 9 cells: 29 dps per sub. Five subs kill a 1000 HP conyard in about 7 s. Splash spares dived subs; only the Sunrise/Atomic blast path can touch one.
**Impact:** Authority, Directorate and Left Behind have no answer to coastal sieges by dived subs. D-143 was decided when only the Expedition was at sea.
**Fix (owner decision):** give land factions a sonar source and a submerged-capable weapon, or reveal a sub after it fires. Size M.

### FACTION-LE-06 · major · SIM · high
**What:** exp_missile_sub has `armor = "submerged"` at all times; there is a TODO about it at vehicles.toml:784-801. ArmorAt (Actors.cs:79) has no dive check.
**Effect:**
- Every exp_ warhead and the Salvo warhead do 0 % vs submerged, so a surfaced sub takes the minimum 1 damage per hit.
- The depth charge targets only dived subs.
- In an Expedition mirror a surfaced sub is near-invulnerable.
**Fix:** give it ship_light armour and use submerged only while dived (one line in ArmorAt). Size S.

### FACTION-LE-07 · major · NUMBERS · high
**What:** auth_, dir_ and lb_ warheads were padded with 100 for ship_light, ship_heavy and submerged (warheads.toml:2-4). D-124 decided only the target mask, not damage values.
**Numbers:**
- Scavenger: 1.9 dps vs a heavy tank, 9.3 vs a ship_heavy cruiser.
- Rotor: 3.5 vs 17.5. Four Rotors kill a 1200 HP cruiser in about 17 s.
- The Expedition's own small arms use 30 % against ship_heavy.
**Impact:** warships and tenders are soft against three factions. Together with LE-04, raiding convoys is very cheap.
**Fix:** fill those three columns deliberately for the 25 non-Expedition warheads, then measure. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-08 · major · SIM · high
**What:** lb_tow has `tow_bonus_permille = 1000` (vehicles.toml:476). Effects.cs:100 zeroes a tow of exactly 1000, so towing has no effect.
**Context:** packed rigs move at 0.8 cells/s, the slowest ground unit; the Tow Truck moves at 1.9.
**History:** the value is in s9_tables:175 and was confirmed in D-134, but its effect is nil. The data test (LeftBehindDataTests:236) only asserts > 0.
**Fix:** set e.g. 2000, and add a test that a towed rig moves faster. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-09 · major · DATA-REF · high
**What:** the Left Behind campaign is capped at 20 supply (one Mother Rig) all the way through. No lb_still_rig is granted or pre-placed anywhere in data/missions. The Authority and Directorate campaigns pre-place supply buildings, and their docs say the player "cannot field the force" without them.
**Never granted in the LB campaign:** still rig, hauler (salvage is never shown), tow, dust sail, refinery rig, scrap wall.
**lb_01:** lists lb_slinger, but it is unbuildable there (no Lookout, no Garage).
**Note:** bot walks still win.
**Fix:** add the still rig to every LB mission (and pre-place it in lb_02-04), and the hauler from lb_02. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-10 · major · DESIGN · high
**What:** units and tooltips promise mechanics that don't exist:
- exp_minelayer: 500 credits, no weapon, no mine code at all.
- exp_diver: can't enter water, and does 0 % vs units (minimum 1). The Archive says it swims deep water.
- exp_destroyer: no depth charge and no AA; its Archive says "DEPTH CHARGE AND GUN".
- exp_launch: can only attack dived subs; its Archive mentions a twin machine gun.
- exp_drydock: produces nothing and repairs nothing ("Heavy ship repair"); it is just a 2000-credit gate.
- Designation (pathfinder, gyro, plotting room) and survey "ash richness" don't exist.
- exp_skyhook has no vehicle_slots.
- lb_harpoon_wagon has no tether; lb_up_grapple has no wall breach.
**Fix:** hide or arm the minelayer, give the Skyhook vehicle_slots = 2, and reword the Archive text. Size S text/data; L for the actual mechanics.

### FACTION-LE-11 · major · DESIGN · medium
**What:** the only Left Behind weapon that can hit air is the static Flak Net (tier 2; 9.6 dps vs light armour, so about 27 s per Gunship, within 5 cells). The Rotor targets ground only.
**Impact:** Left Behind field armies can't answer Expedition Gunships, Bombers or Jets, Authority bombers or Directorate dirigibles. Design 06 doesn't say this is intended.
**Fix (owner decision):** a "both"-target Slinger or Harpoon, or a flak wagon.

### FACTION-LE-12 · major · NUMBERS · medium
**What:** repair auras heal 1 % of the target's max HP per 10 ticks, free and while under fire (Effects.cs:164-168). Only the best single source counts, and aura_targets is ignored.
**Numbers:**
- A Mender (550) heals a Carrier 30 HP/s, a Cruiser 24 HP/s, a packed rig 20 HP/s.
- Destroyer gun vs ship_heavy is 18 dps, Rotor 17.5, so one Mender cancels about 1.7 attackers on a Carrier.
- A Tinker can heal a packed rig for free, and it unpacks at the healed ratio, which beats building repair (which costs 50 %).
**Fix:** cap the heal in HP or make it flat; charge for packed-rig repair; honour aura_targets. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-13 · minor · SIM · high
**What:** upgrades do less than their text:
- Salvage Rights only speeds the drain (SalvageMission.cs:59). Wreck value never changes, despite "wreck value x1.5" in the data and Archive.
- Grapple's ×1.5 is applied before the 1000 plunder cap (Raid.cs:65-67): nothing gained once the victim stores 5000+.
- Patchwork: Tinkers already repair at radius 3; it only adds 2 cells.
- Sonar Nets raises picket sight only. sonar_radius isn't an effect attribute (EffectRulesLoader.cs:88), so subs aren't revealed.
- Scrap Plate gives +20 % HP, not armour, and skips the Rotor and Kite.
**Fix:** implement the stated effects or reword the tooltips. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-14 · minor · SIM · high
**What:** the Kite is "one-use" only by accident.
**Detail:** lb_kite_bomb has rate_of_fire 1 (fires every tick, 2400 dps) and no one-use code exists. The Kite dies only because its own hits_allies splash deals 60-120 to its 60 HP; then its death weapon fires again. Any extra effective HP (a vet rank, a buff) would turn it into a reusable 2400-dps flyer.
**Fix:** add an explicit one-use flag and a sane rate of fire. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-15 · minor · SIM · high
**What:** exp_provision_store has `supply_unstocked = 5`, but SupplyUnstocked and stock_intervals are unused (Supply.cs:99-104). The store always gives +10, so D-130 option A (full supply only while shipments arrive) is missing.
**Fix:** implement it with a last-arrival tick, or drop the key. Size S-M.

### FACTION-LE-16 · minor · NUMBERS · high
**What:** lb_refinery_rig is dominated by lb_yard.
**Compare:**
- Yard: 600, storage 3000, power -10, free Gleaner.
- Refinery Rig: 900, storage 600, power -20, no Gleaner.
**Why packing doesn't save it:** the Mother Rig unpacks anywhere and a Yard can then go within 2 cells. Low storage also wastes deliveries (the reason for D-157).
**Fix:** cut the price or add a free Gleaner. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-17 · minor · SIM · high
**What:** the Second Drift storm raises radiation to 64 (the damage threshold is 32).
**Effect:**

- Design says the storm "does not kill outright" and LB units get a stealth bonus; only the Veilwalker does.
- Flak hits_allies splash also hits own ground units under the plane it shoots.
**Fix:** exempt air units from radiation and decide LB storm resistance. Size S.

### FACTION-LE-18 · minor · NUMBERS · medium
**What:** quays (600), shipyards and hover yards all count as factories for the one shared vehicle lane (Production.cs:76-83, 297-306). Hover Yard + 2 Quays halves Glide Tank build time; a quay is the cheapest factory in the game. A cruiser no dock can launch stalls the whole lane (known; only a tooltip warns).
**Fix:** count only factories that can launch the item's move class. Size S.

## Things checked and found sound
- All lb_/exp_ prerequisites resolve within their own faction, and the tier gates are reachable. 28 upgrades (under the 64 limit). All effect targets and attributes exist and are read through RulesOf.
- Every unit field the LB/EX data uses has code behind it, apart from the gaps above.
- Archive entries exist for all ids; sprites exist for all ids except lb_still_rig.
- Rig pack/unpack timing, HP carry-over, site validation, the unit-lane pause and the supply drop while packing all work.
- The Second Drift fires (D-132), and LB units are immune to its slow.
- No infinite salvage loop, apart from the inflated types in LE-04.
- Shipments: decay applies only at launch, the floor and charter work, the tender lane picks the right sea, and selling or rebuilding the Anchorage gives no exploit.
- Beachhead zone and build radius both apply; the floating-dock rule holds; the coast guard covers hand-made and generated maps.
- Losing all landing craft doesn't strand the Expedition.
- Surface ships, tenders and the Command Ship can be hit by every faction's aircraft; only the dived sub is out of reach.
- AI build lists use only real ids; FactionAiKeys covers all four factions.
