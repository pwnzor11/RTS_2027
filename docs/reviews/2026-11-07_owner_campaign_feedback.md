# Owner feedback after playing the campaign (2026-11-07, session 14)

Owner's notes, grouped. Each item gets a roadmap id in M11 (UI and input overhaul) or M10 (art polish).

## UI and input (M11)
1. **Objectives:** hard to tell where the mission wants you to go.
2. **Screen clutter:** the control hints are dumped on screen. Move them to a Controls page in Settings.
3. **Camera:** the map is hard to navigate. Mouse at the screen edge should scroll the view that way (edge scrolling).
4. **Unit size:** units look very small on the map (scale / zoom).
5. **Selection info:** single-unit info (health, kills, etc.) sits at the top left. Owner wants it in its own panel, probably on the right with
   the build menu, shrinking the build menu while a unit is selected and restoring it after. Owner is open to suggestions.
6. **Overall:** take a long look at the UI to make it more user-friendly and modern, and an adversarial look at the whole user-input path.

## Readability (M11 where it is UI/contrast, M10 where it is art)
7. **Terrain:** the map looks one-dimensional; you can't tell what is a path, water, mountain, etc.
8. **Resources:** look like a splatter of colour.
9. **Building animations:** too fast; busy and frantic.

## Art polish later (M10, owner: "save that for a later polish run")
10. Buildings still look flat and one-dimensional.
