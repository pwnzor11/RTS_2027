# M6-10: Left Behind AI (Raider Warlord)

## Method
`Tools ai match --a tiered:<n> --b tiered:<m> --faction-a left_behind --faction-b <faction> --seeds 16 --first {1,17} --ticks 25000 --size 56`
run twice per cell (seeds 1-16 and 17-32, each seed played twice with the starts swapped) = **64 games per cell**. Release build of
`src/Tools` (the `--faction-*` options now also accept `left_behind`). Base-branch data (before the generic-veterancy change on integ-s9).
Cells 4v3 and the Authority cell were measured after the last tuning of the aircraft choice; the final small tweaks (relocation site
fallback, Hauler threshold 250 -> 150) came after the table and were checked only with the stuck-rig test.

## Results (A = Left Behind unless noted)
| Cell | Games | A wins | B wins | Draws | A win % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LB 3 vs Authority 3 | 64 | 12 | 48 | 4 | 19% |
| LB 3 vs Directorate 3 | 64 | 38 | 8 | 18 | 59% |
| LB ladder 2 (A) vs 1 (B) | 64 | 48 | 5 | 11 | 75% |
| LB ladder 4 (A) vs 3 (B) | 64 | 27 | 29 | 8 | 42% |
| LB mirror 3 vs 3 | 64 | 23 | 29 | 12 | 36% (slot/start effect) |

Before the aircraft change tier 4 beat tier 3 in only 15 of 64 (23%): the one-use Kites (500 each) and the unfireable Second Drift were
a drain. The AI now prefers the Rotor gunship and does not build a superweapon it cannot fire.

## Observations (stuck-rig test, 4 games of 14000 ticks; counters from the AI)
- Unpacks: the Mother Rig unpacks on the first decision (1 order in clean games), the base (Yard, Dynamo, Muster, Garage, Spike Nest,
  Lookout) stands within about 2500 ticks, no unplaceable Place commands.
- Raids: 13-37 raid orders per game; Hook Runners and Buggies set out in parties of 3+ against the nearest known Silo/Refinery, else probe
  the enemy start.
- Salvage: works when a Hauler exists (15 orders in one game, wreck value drained in the scripted test), but wrecks are small and decay in
  60 s, so most games buy no Hauler (0-1 orders). Tune: lower the value gate or build the Hauler earlier.
- Relocation (tier 3+): triggers on a heavy attack (about 3 decisions of enemy gunmen worth 900+ and 1.5x our home army); 2 of 3 triggered
  relocations finished, 1 was abandoned on the timeout. Before the site fallback, rigs stood packed and idle up to 1760 ticks; now the
  worst idle-packed stretch is 420 ticks. No rig ends a game packed. Tier 1-2 never relocate (test).
- The storm: **never fired** (see QUESTIONS in the report): `lb_second_drift` carries `superweapon_entrance_range = 8` copied from the
  Sunrise, and Left Behind has no tunnel entrance building, so `CanFireSuperweapon` is always false. The AI therefore skips building it.
  The targeting code (army cluster, else base cluster, then raiders chained onto the nearest Silo) is written but untested in a match.
- Draws: 4-18 of 64 per cell (the mirror and the Directorate cell are highest); draws are games where neither base falls in 25000 ticks.
