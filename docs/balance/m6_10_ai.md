# M6-10: Left Behind AI (Raider Warlord)

## Method
`Tools ai match --a tiered:<n> --b tiered:<m> --faction-a left_behind --faction-b <faction> --seeds 16 --first {1,17} --ticks 25000 --size 56`
run twice per cell (seeds 1-16 and 17-32, each seed played twice with the starts swapped) = **64 games per cell**. Release build of
`src/Tools` (the `--faction-*` options now also accept `left_behind`). Base-branch data (before the generic-veterancy change on integ-s9).
Cells 4v3 and the Authority cell were measured after the last tuning of the aircraft choice; the final small tweaks (relocation site
fallback, Hauler threshold 250 -> 150) came after the table and were checked only with the stuck-rig test.

Before the aircraft change tier 4 beat tier 3 in only 15 of 64 (23%): the one-use Kites (500 each) and the unfireable Second Drift were
a drain. The AI now prefers the Rotor gunship and does not build a superweapon it cannot fire.

## Observations (stuck-rig test, 4 games of 14000 ticks; counters from the AI)
- Unpacks: the Mother Rig unpacks on the first decision (1 order in clean games), the base (Yard, Dynamo, Muster, Garage, Spike Nest,
  Lookout) stands within about 2500 ticks, no unplaceable Place commands.
- Raids: 13-37 raid orders per game; Hook Runners and Buggies set out in parties of 3+ against the nearest known Silo/Refinery, else probe
  the enemy start.
- Salvage: works when a Hauler exists (15 orders in one game, wreck value drained in the scripted test), but wrecks are small and decay in
  60 s, so most games buy no Hauler (0-1 orders). Tune: lower the value gate or build the Hauler earlier.
- Relocation (tier 3+): triggers on a heavy attack (about 3 decisions of enemy gunmen worth 900+ and 1.5x our home army); 2 of 3 triggered
  relocations finished, 1 was abandoned on the timeout. Before the site fallback, rigs stood packed and idle up to 1760 ticks; now the
  worst idle-packed stretch is 420 ticks. No rig ends a game packed. Tier 1-2 never relocate (test).
- The storm: **never fired** (see QUESTIONS in the report): `lb_second_drift` carries `superweapon_entrance_range = 8` copied from the
  Sunrise, and Left Behind has no tunnel entrance building, so `CanFireSuperweapon` is always false. The AI therefore skips building it.
  The targeting code (army cluster, else base cluster, then raiders chained onto the nearest Silo) is written but untested in a match.
- Draws: 4-18 of 64 per cell (the mirror and the Directorate cell are highest); draws are games where neither base falls in 25000 ticks.

## Session 10 re-measure (supply + veterancy + VET-6)
Same cells on the integ-s10 data (supply cap ON, generic veterancy, the VET-6 AI), Release build of `src/Tools`, **32 games per cell**
(seeds 1-16, each played twice with the starts swapped; 25000 ticks, size 56). With 32 games the resolution is about **+-9 points** (one
standard deviation at 50 %), so only differences of 15+ points mean anything. "Before" is the same binary without the session-10 Left
Behind change (measured now, same seeds); "Session 9" is the 64-game table above, which is on older data and not comparable.

AI changes (src/AI/Recruit/LeftBehindTactics.cs): the first Hauler is bought as soon as 5 army units stand and the first Gleaner works,
with no wreck needed (a second one at tier 3+ once wrecks lie about); wreck gate 150 -> 60 `(tune)`; a lost Hauler is not rebought for
3600 ticks and none is bought below 900 funds `(tune)`; a Hauler out on the ash field (mission Harvest) is taken for a wreck; a wreck
next to enemy gunmen counts as safe when our own armed units there are worth at least as much (a won fight leaves wrecks beside the
winners); a Hauler working the field is no longer sent a Return order every 400 ticks. That last one was a bug exposed by the others: with
it the Directorate cell fell from 50 % to 12 %, without it the cell is level with before. Wreck decay, wreck values and Hauler stats are
unchanged.

Reading it:
- Salvage now runs in every game (Hauler in 32/32 games, was 0-1 orders per game before; about 5-9 orders and 190-380 ash per game per side).
  "Ash drained" is the drop in wreck value beyond the wrecks' own linear decay, attributed to the nearest salvager within 3 cells; it is
  an under-estimate and is not the credits banked (the hopper pays when it docks; the Hauler costs 500, so one Hauler pays for itself
  only after about 2-3 fought-over wrecks of that size).
- Win rates: the Hauler does not buy wins by itself. Within the +-9 noise every cell is level with "Before" except the ladder 2v1 (+22) and
  the mirror (-16, but that cell is a slot/start effect: A wins 59 % or 43 % of identical-AI games; read it as 50 +- 10). The Authority cell stays at 12 % and the tier-4 cell at about 21 %:
  those are about the army, not the wreck income. The big move against Session 9 (59 -> 40 in the Directorate cell, 42 -> 21 at 4v3)
  comes from the new base data (supply cap, veterancy), not from this change: "Before" already shows it.
- The 4v3 ladder (tier 4 should beat tier 3) is still lost (21 %), though it was 25 % before the change. Tier 4 buys the Second Drift
  (2 500) and Rotors; why that does not pay off was not investigated (a guess: the 2 500 and the supply cap slow the army). Worth a look in
  a later round; not changed here.
- The Second Drift now fires in real matches: 13 orders in 32 tier-4 games (it needs the Rotor Pad, 2 500 credits and 4800 ticks of charge,
  so many games end first). The test `TheSecondDriftIsFiredInARealMatch` stops a real match at the first order and checks that a storm
  stands on the map (seed 2 of 6). The storm itself was not measured for its effect on the result.
- Tooling: `Tools ai match ... --extras 1` prints per game and per side: Hauler built (y/n), salvage orders, ash drained, Second Drift orders.
