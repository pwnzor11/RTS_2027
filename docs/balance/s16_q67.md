# S16 Q-67: Directorate tiers 2-3 early game (D-191, AI only)

Method: Release build of `src/Tools` per variant in a scratch dir; `ai match --a tiered:A --b tiered:B --faction-a F --faction-b F --seeds 16 --size 96 --ticks 30000`
(32 games per cell, A = first faction/tier, starts swapped). Probes used 8 seeds. Games are deterministic: the shipped code reproduced the probe cell game for game.
Noise: about +-10 points on a decisive % at 30 decisive games, +-3 on a draw count. "Dec %" = A wins / (A wins + B wins).

## Diagnosis

`ai timeline --trace 300`, seed 1, Left Behind 3 v Directorate 3: Left Behind has 20 units at t1500 (Directorate 14), and Directorate sits at 7-12 units through t3000 with
cash near 0 for long stretches, then 2-3 units at t6000 while Left Behind keeps 33. Directorate 3 v Authority 3 (seed 1): Directorate 17 units at t3000 but 6 at t3600 after a
lost fight, with the tier 2 gate bought at about t4200. In the build order the Isotope Works (800) and Rad Mortar (700) come right after the Pillbox, so about 1 500 credits
of tech land before there is an army. The shared tier 3 profile climbs by clock (tier-2 gate at 3 min), not by army.

## The change (src/AI, Directorate tiers 2-3 only)

`TierPlanner.DirectorateTechArmy = 18`: for the Directorate at difficulty tiers 2 and 3, the build-order steps Isotope Works and Rad Mortar are skipped (the existing `HoldStep`
hook) until army + home guard + detached units alive reach 18. Tier 1, tier 4, tier 5 and the other factions never see it (set in `RecruitAi.Init`), so those cells stay bit-identical.
No data or number change; no new long-lived field (the rule reads the live army). Test: `DirectorateTechHoldTests`.

## Reading
- Left Behind 3 v Directorate 3 reaches the 50-65 % band (59 %) and draws fall 9 -> 3. Directorate 1 v Authority 1, Directorate 5 v Authority 5 and the control are bit-identical.
- Directorate 3 v Authority 3 improves from 44 % to 65 %: at the edge of the "under 65 %" limit.
- Misses: Directorate 3 v Expedition 3 falls from 50 % to 41 % (inside noise, wrong direction), and Directorate 5 v Directorate 3 falls from 36 % to 28 % decisive
  (5 / 13 / 14): a stronger tier 3 makes the tier 5 gap larger. Tier 5 was not touched; it simply meets a better tier 3.
- The three holds trace one line: the stronger the tier 3, the lower Left Behind and Expedition and the higher Authority; no value satisfies every row.

## Options for the owner
- Accept hold 18 (Left Behind in band, Authority at 65 %, Expedition and tier 5 slightly down).
- Hold 16: Authority 57 %, Expedition 47 %, tier 5 v 3 31 %, but Left Behind only 71 % decisive (misses the band).
- Hold 20: Left Behind 52 % and 1 draw, but Authority 77 % and Expedition 30 %.
- A tier 5 fix (separate package): Directorate tier 5 loses to a tier 3 that now keeps its tech back; its early army is the same lever.
