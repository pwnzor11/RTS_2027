# S15 Q-65: Directorate tier 4-5 and Left Behind tier 3 (D-182, AI changes only)

Method: Release build of `src/Tools` per variant in a scratch dir; `ai match --seeds 16 --size 96 --ticks 30000` (32 games per cell, A = first faction/tier),
traces with `ai timeline --trace 1500`. Probe runs of 8 seeds (16 games) were used to screen knobs through a temporary environment override (removed again).
Noise: about +-18 points on a decisive %, +-3 on a draw count.

## Diagnosis

**Directorate tier 5 v Authority 5 (seed 1 trace).** At t1500 both sides are even (14 v 5 units). From t2400 the Authority side has twice the income
(cumulative 3 569 v 1 629 at t2400, 12 291 v 9 422 at t4800, 29 852 v 20 390 at t12000) and 2x the army (29 v 13 at t4500). Cause: the tier 5 profile has
`expansion_tick = 2400`, but `TierPlanner.ExtendBuild` appended the second Smelter Vent to the END of the build order, behind pillbox, Isotope Works and
Rad Mortar (and the tech structures the tier 5 flags add), so with cash at 0 for most of the game the second refinery and the second Scraper arrived late
(first Bore at t2892, Isotope Works t3084, Rad Mortar t3972, Hatch t5744, Redoubt t9012). The Authority order has its single refinery in the first three steps. Tier 1-3 Directorate
(expansion tick 0 or 4800, no air) never hit this ordering, which is why the problem only shows at tiers 4-5.
Probes on the 8-seed v Authority 5 cell (decisive wins of 16 games, base 1 win): harvester_target 2 / 3 = 2 / 3 wins; superweapon + air off = 3; harass off = 2;
two_pronged/multi_front/adapt off = 1 (no effect); expansion tick 1200 with target 3 = 4; refinery moved up in the build order (final change) = 9 wins of 16.
Unit mix, harvester count and wave knobs moved the result by a game or two each; the build order position of the expansion refinery moved it by about 8.

**Directorate v Left Behind 5.** Same economy lag, plus Left Behind tier 5 out-produces everything (see below); the fix lifts the cell from 6 % to 18 % decisive, not further.

**Left Behind tier 3.** It reaches an army of 20 at t1500 and 42 at t3000 against 5 and 14, because its fixed refinery and silo, free Gleaner and cheap Buggy/Hook Runner
bags give it the fastest start of any faction; the shared tier 3 profile only caps how late it fights. Probes (8 seeds v Authority 3, A decisive %): base 11 / 4 / 1 (68 %); no expansion/no upgrades 62 %;
attack_threshold 14 = 81 %; attack_ratio 160 = 62 %; enemy-aware mix off, two-pronged off, counter-defences off = 68 %; harvesters 1 = 62 %; attack_threshold 4 = 50 %.
Only the wave trigger moved it down. On 16 seeds trigger 4 gave 53 % v Authority 3 but made Left Behind 3 WIN MORE v Directorate 3 (81 %, from about 85 %: no move) and cost the Left Behind
tier ladder (5 v 3 fell from 34 % to 21 %, with trigger 6: 18 %), because tier 5 shares the same early build and its waves are the ones that are bigger and later. So a tier 3 wave-trigger
change is not safe for the ladder and was NOT kept.

## What changed
- `TierPlanner.ExtendBuild` (Directorate with the air flag, i.e. tiers 4-5): the expansion refinery is inserted directly after the Assembly Bore (new `BuildOrderManager.InsertAfter`)
  instead of at the end of the order. Other factions and Directorate tiers 1-3 are unchanged (tier 1-3 Directorate has no air flag, so the old path is kept). No new long-lived state (the order is already saved/restored).
- Left Behind tier 3: no code change shipped. A profile-only change (attack_threshold) is shared with Authority/Directorate in data/ai/difficulty.toml and does not touch only Left Behind; see below.

## Reading
- Directorate tier 5 v Authority 5 reached the target band (46 % decisive, was 23 %; base 5 wins -> 11). v Left Behind 5 improved (9 -> 23 %) but stays under the 35 % aim: Left Behind tier 5 is simply faster.
- Tier ladder: Directorate 5 v 3 is still weak (5 wins v 9, 18 draws), not made worse beyond the noise; the draw count rose by 4 (14 -> 18) which breaks "no new draw spike" within noise. The tier-5 Directorate still plays below its tier 3 in the mirror-ish cell.
- Left Behind tier 3 remains too strong (68 % v Authority 3, 87 % v Directorate 3). The only knob that moved it, the wave trigger, hurts Left Behind's own tier ladder. Without changing unit/building numbers or tier 5 behaviour
  the next options are: a Left Behind-specific cap on tier 3 production (a `[faction.left_behind]` key in difficulty.toml and loader support in src/Data, which this package does not own), or lowering tier 3 harvester/early-army pace only
  (a faction-keyed build delay). Measured numbers for owner decision: wave trigger 8 (shipped) 68 %, 4 = 50 %, 14 = 81 % v Authority 3; tier 5 v tier 3 at trigger 4/6/8: 21 / 18 / 34 %.
