# Units

One entry per rules id (19). Format: `title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, `field_note:`, body. Authority field notes read as
teletype; Directorate field notes as Voice broadcast. Names are working names (design 05 section 5).

## Authority

### auth_rifleman
title: Rifleman
subtitle: Citizen-army infantry
unlock: open
field_note: FIELD MAN 1 // SERVING CITIZEN. HOLDS GROUND. REQUIRES DECON AFTER ASHFALL EXPOSURE. STOP.

Raised from the barracks by the labour-year draft, the rifleman is a clerk, a lineworker or a farmhand with a service rifle
and a month of drill. He is not a hero, and the Authority does not ask him to be one. He is asked to hold a position until
the armour arrives, and to stay out of the ash.

### auth_rocketeer
title: Rocketeer
subtitle: Anti-armour infantry
unlock: open
field_note: ROCKET SECTION 2 // HEAVY AND SLOW. KEEP BEHIND THE LINE. DO NOT WASTE ROUNDS. STOP.

Trained at the same barracks as riflemen but on tube-guided launchers that need a steady hand and a clear lane. Each rocket
is hand-counted into the inventory and signed for on return. Rocketeers are the Authority's answer to anything armoured
that gets too close.

### auth_scraper
title: Scraper
subtitle: Armoured ashfall harvester
unlock: open
field_note: SCRAPER 3 // SEALED CAB, FULL HOPPER. RETURN TO REFINERY AT DUSK. STOP.

A squat armoured crawler built at the Vehicle Factory, with a plough blade, a sealed cab and a lead-lined hopper. Its crew
are the most respected workers in a reconstruction town, since every ration depends on how full the hopper comes back.
Drivers paint the names of their children on the hull.

### auth_jeep
title: Scout Car
subtitle: Light recon vehicle
unlock: open
field_note: LIGHT PATROL CAR 4 // FAST, THIN, USEFUL. REPORT WHAT YOU SEE, NOT WHAT YOU FEAR. STOP.

A wheeled scout with a pintle-mounted machine gun and a radio rack taller than the driver. Surveyors use them to find the
edge of the next good field; the army uses them to find the enemy before the enemy finds the column.

### auth_tank
title: Heavy Tank
subtitle: Main battle armour
unlock: open
field_note: ARMOUR 5 // MAIN GUN, TRACKED, REACTOR CORE SEALED. DO NOT FOLLOW IT INTO ITS OWN SMOKE. STOP.

The backbone of the Authority's armoured columns, a slab-sided tracked tank with a small sealed reactor and a long
cannon. They are built slowly, serviced obsessively and rarely lost. Crews say the hull plate is thick because the
Administrator is cheap and wants his tanks back.

### auth_decon_truck
title: Decontamination Truck
subtitle: Clean-up and ground restoration
unlock: campaign
field_note: DECON UNIT 6 // SPRAY, FLUSH, REPEAT. ONE CLEAN CELL IS ONE LESS WAY TO LOSE A MAN. STOP.

A wheeled tanker with spray booms and a neutralising rinse. Decon crews follow the army in blue overalls and are the
reason an infantry company can cross poisoned ground. The Authority treats cleaning up after a battle as part of the battle.

## Directorate

### dir_conscript
title: Conscript
subtitle: Citizen in service
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "CITIZENS IN SERVICE, YOUR SHIFT IS GREAT AND YOUR DEBT IS SMALL. REPORT TO THE HATCH."

Mustered by the hundreds from the lower levels, conscripts carry a stamped rifle, a respirator that is mostly for show and
the belief that the surface was stolen from them. They are cheap, quick and numerous, and the Directorate does not pretend
otherwise. Most of them have never seen a sunrise and are very curious about it.

### dir_ashthrower
title: Ash Thrower
subtitle: Radiation infantry
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE GROUND REMEMBERS OUR DEBTS. LET IT COLLECT."

A conscript with a pressure tank and a lance, throwing concentrated ash across the ground in front of the line. Volunteers
are given a hot meal and a longer service credit. They also have a short life expectancy, and the Commissariat is
not shy about it.

### dir_sapper
title: Sapper
subtitle: Demolition infantry
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "WALLS ARE ONLY A POLITE REQUEST. THE SAPPER ANSWERS."

Sappers come from the shaft crews who dig and prop the Annex's own tunnels. With a collapse charge on a pole they
undermine a wall or gate, and the structure does the rest. The Directorate considers this the most respectable trade
below ground.

### dir_informant
title: Informant
subtitle: Stealth observer
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "A GOOD CITIZEN IS ALWAYS PRESENT. A BETTER CITIZEN IS NEVER NOTICED."

Trained by the Commissariat to lie in the ash for hours and report on what they see. They carry no weapon, only a handset
and a very quiet disposition. Their reports feed the purges as often as the battle plan, which is why other conscripts
are polite to them.

### dir_commissar
title: Commissar
subtitle: Officer of Order
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE COMMISSARIAT SEES WHAT YOU WILL DO BEFORE YOU DO IT."

An officer of the Commissariat of Order, in a long coat and a respirator mask. Commissars arrive with the second wave, hold the line
by presence and keep a pistol for those who forget they are being watched. The conscripts fight harder near one, and not
only out of loyalty.

### dir_scraper
title: Burrowing Scraper
subtitle: Tunnel-borne ashfall harvester
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "THE SURFACE OWES US A HARVEST. COLLECT IT, CITIZEN, AND COME HOME."

Built at the Assembly Bore, this scraper tunnels to a drift, surfaces, gathers ash and returns to the Smelter Vent below.
It is cheaper and faster to field than the Authority's version and far easier to kill. The Directorate counts that as
a fair trade.

### dir_bore
title: Bore
subtitle: Entrance digger
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "A HATCH WHERE THERE WAS NONE. THE INTERIOR EXTENDS ITS HAND."

A drill-nosed tracked machine that grinds to a chosen spot, then deploys into a new Hatch. Bore crews are the closest thing
the Directorate has to explorers, and they talk with a swagger that the Commissariat politely ignores.

### dir_mole
title: Mole
subtitle: Burrowing transport
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "FIVE CITIZENS, ONE FINE DARK ROAD. NOBODY WILL SEE YOU ARRIVE."

An armoured burrowing carrier that takes five conscripts under the ground and puts them out where they are needed. Mole
drivers are known for gallows humour and for never arriving where the Authority expects.

### dir_agitprop
title: Agitprop Crawler
subtitle: Loudspeaker support vehicle
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "LISTEN, CITIZENS, AND BE STEADY. THE INTERIOR IS WITH YOU."

A tracked vehicle bristling with horn speakers, carrying the Voice into the field. It steadies and hurries nearby
conscripts and floods radio bands so Authority scouts cannot read a map. It is easy to dislike, which is partly the point.

### dir_reactor_crawler
title: Reactor Crawler
subtitle: Heavy radiation vehicle
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE BRIGHT SERVANT OF THE INTERIOR. DO NOT STAND CLOSE, CITIZEN. STAND PROUD."

A slow, heavy crawler whose bare reactor core is the weapon. It throws radiation across the ground in front of it and
is shielded for its crew only. When destroyed it melts down, and its crews know this. Nobody volunteers to drive one,
and nobody who has driven one is ever teased about it.

### dir_rocket_truck
title: Rocket Truck
subtitle: Mobile rocket artillery
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "SKY AND GROUND ALIKE ARE OURS TO RECLAIM. FIRE WHEN THE LAMP TURNS RED."

A fast truck with a rack of unguided rockets that can be aimed at ground or aircraft. Crews chalk a tally of kills on the cab,
and the Commissariat does not stop them. The rockets are loaded at the Muster Hatch from a supply that is shorter than
the Voice admits.

### dir_dirigible
title: Dirigible
subtitle: Scouting airship
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE INTERIOR HAS EYES UPON THE HEAVENS. THE SKY IS NO LONGER A STRANGER."

A slow, fragile envelope hung with a gondola, launched from a Mooring Tower. It sees farther than any ground unit and can drop
canisters of Blackout smoke that blind the enemy's own scouts. It is the closest the Directorate has to an air force, and
crews who fly it do so mostly to see the sky.

### dir_landtrain
title: Land-Train
subtitle: Mobile command and rail gun
unlock: encounter
field_note: VOICE: "THE SEAT OF GOVERNMENT IS WHEREVER THE INTERIOR RIDES. BOW TO IT, OR BE RUN OVER."

A tracked train of linked armoured cars with a rail gun on the lead wagon. When it halts, it deploys into a Command Shaft and
the Directorate has a base where there was none. Authority tacticians fear it more than any single weapon, because it
makes the Directorate's front line wherever it likes.
