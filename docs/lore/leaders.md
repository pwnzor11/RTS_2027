# Leaders (DRAFT for owner review)

Entry ids use the prefix `leader_`. Each entry: `title:` (name), `subtitle:` (role), `unlock:`, then bio, motivation, and
a `secret:` paragraph that the Archive reveals later (suggest unlock on the named mission; here marked `unlock: campaign`
for the entry and the secret released with it by the lead). All names are invented.

## Authority

### leader_auth_wrenfield
title: Brigadier Hester Wrenfield
subtitle: Field commander of the Authority
unlock: open

A rail-signals officer who rose in the labour-year militia, Wrenfield speaks to the commander over the field radio in
short, dry sentences. She has buried more convoy drivers than she cares to count and plans every operation as if she
were paying for it personally.

Motivation: bring the convoys home. She does not hate the Directorate; she wants it to stop costing her people.

Secret: Her own brother was among the Glass Coast evacuees the Authority left behind in 1990, and she has read the
decision memo. She follows orders anyway, and has quietly kept a list.

### leader_auth_pell
title: Administrator Josiah Pell
subtitle: Civilian administrator of the Authority
unlock: open

Pell is a county drainage engineer who became Administrator because no one else would sign the budget. Soft-spoken and
exact, he sets the commander's objectives by teletype and refuses to describe any operation as a victory.

Motivation: keep the Charter intact. A war that ends with emergency powers is, to him, a loss.

Secret: His office has held the 1974 "hum" file for decades. He knew the Annex existed and chose not to dig, because
digging meant admitting the Authority was not the only government left.

### leader_auth_dray
title: Lieutenant Anselm Dray
subtitle: The Directorate defector
unlock: campaign

A conscript-engineer from Shaft Four, Dray crossed the lines carrying a reactor log and a question: why does the Voice
read out numbers for a population the Annex no longer has? He is clever, awkward in daylight, and refuses to wear
a weapon.

Motivation: to get the people below out before the Annex runs out of air.

Secret: He helped design the Sunrise Device's tunnel head. He believes he alone can find where its warheads are buried and
he has told no one how he knows.

### leader_auth_fenn
title: Chief Surveyor Imogen Fenn
subtitle: Survey and logistics lead
unlock: campaign

Fenn mapped the Glass Coast launch sites on foot in the Great Drift of 1990. She is blunt, tireless and has opinions
about everyone's boots.

Motivation: finish the map, so nobody else walks into those sites blind.

Secret: Her survey also found a sealed Authority depot beneath one site, with a stockpile of pre-Exchange weapons that
Pell's office ordered to stay unrecorded.

## Directorate

### leader_dir_harrowgate
title: Director Lucan Harrowgate
subtitle: The Director of the Interior
unlock: open

The Director is the face on every poster: tall, tired eyes, a hand raised in measured greeting. In the Annex he is
described as a man who gave up the sun so that others might keep their lives. His recorded addresses are played at dawn
shift in every level.

Motivation, as told: reclaim what was held in trust.

Secret: He died in the winter of 1974. The addresses since then have been assembled from his old recordings and rewritten
by a committee. The Voice knows. The Commissar chairs it.

### leader_dir_larkin
title: The Voice (Odette Larkin)
subtitle: The Director's announcer
unlock: open

Once a morning radio announcer, Larkin has read the Director's orders for thirty years in a voice so reassuring that
some conscripts will walk into fire if she says they are loved. Off the air she is wry, careful and afraid.

Motivation: keep the Annex calm, because panic below kills faster than ash above.

Secret: She has been quietly adding true weather reports and small lullabies to the scripts, and she has twice
rewritten an order to spare a battalion. If the Commissar finds out, she disappears.

### leader_dir_thrall
title: Commissar Ignatius Thrall
subtitle: Commissar of Order
unlock: campaign

Thrall gives the orders that the Voice announces. Precise, courteous and unhurried, he keeps a notebook of names and
has never raised his voice. In the Annex, a visit from the Commissariat is spoken of the way people once spoke of
winter.

Motivation: preserve the Annex, at any cost to everyone who lives in it.

Secret: Thrall has found the Director's standing instruction, Order Seven, written in 1963: if the surface refuses to submit,
the warheads under the launch sites are to be detonated. He is the only one who believes the Director would still want
it, and he is preparing the Sunrise Device.

### leader_dir_lund
title: Dr. Perpetua Lund
subtitle: Chief of the Isotope Works
unlock: campaign

Lund runs the Directorate's reactors and weapons programme. Intelligent, lonely, impatient with slogans, she is the person
who actually keeps the Annex warm. She rarely leaves the lower levels.

Motivation: keep the reactors alive. She will build whatever the Commissar asks as long as it buys fuel.

Secret: She has known for years that the Annex cannot survive another decade, and she has been quietly preparing a way up
that does not need a war. She needs someone the Commissar cannot see.
