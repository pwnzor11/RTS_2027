# The Continental Reconstruction Authority (DRAFT for owner review)

Entry ids use the prefix `faction_auth_`. Format per entry: `title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, body.

### faction_auth_society
title: A Government of Clerks and Engineers
subtitle: Society and government of the Authority
unlock: open

The Authority is governed by councils of the people who keep things running: grid engineers, rail dispatchers,
agricultural surveyors, county clerks. Each district elects a Reconstruction Board, the boards send delegates to the
Administration, and the Administration answers to a Charter that nobody is allowed to rewrite on a Tuesday. Decisions
are slow, minuted and filed in triplicate, and the Authority is proud of it. Slow is how it survived the First Winter
without a single coup.

Its people are not united by a leader or a flag but by a ledger. Everyone is counted; everyone is owed a ration, a
roof and a hearing. The price is a labour year, served by every citizen in work crews, convoy service or the militia.

### faction_auth_daily_life
title: Daily Life Inside the Walls
subtitle: Work, ration, decon
unlock: campaign

A day in a reconstruction town starts with the whistle, the roll call and a decon shower, because ash gets into
everything. Bread is baked from grain grown under glass, news arrives as a teletype strip pinned on the depot wall,
and the evening is for the district hall, where people argue about drainage with real passion. Children learn
arithmetic on slide rules and read the Charter aloud before they read anything else.

The towns are stencilled in grey-green: pipework, painted unit numbers, hand-lettered signs. Anything not bolted down is
borrowed from the common pool and returned on time. Fear is real, but it is expressed as maintenance.

### faction_auth_values
title: What the Authority Is Fighting For
subtitle: The reluctant army
unlock: open

The Authority does not want a war. It wants a working grid, a census that adds up, and fields it can plant. Its first
principle is that the surface belongs to whoever tends it, and its second is that the people who tend it should be
allowed to go home afterwards. The Charter still contains the clause that will dissolve the Authority when its work is
done, and the soldiers know it.

That reluctance is also a weakness. The Authority will fortify, repair and wait long past the point where an enemy
would have attacked, and it will count the cost of every shell before it fires one.

### faction_auth_army
title: The Citizen Army
subtitle: How the Authority fights
unlock: campaign

The army is the labour year with rifles. Riflemen and rocketeers are citizens in service, supported by engineers, decon
crews and the convoy drivers who keep them supplied. Its doctrine is the surveyor's: find the ground, hold the ground,
then improve the ground. A base has a perimeter, a power grid and a road out. Armour goes forward behind sealed
transports because ash kills unprotected infantry, and every column carries clean water.

It is slow and expensive, and it is very hard to dislodge. Its air arm is the last thing it rebuilt, and its artillery is
the best on the continent, because the Authority never lost the habit of doing the sums.

### faction_auth_symbols
title: Marks and Colours
subtitle: Symbols of the Authority
unlock: open

The Authority's mark is a toothed gear around a compass rose, stencilled on vehicle doors, depot walls and ration
tins. Its colours are working grey, olive green and safety yellow for anything that might move. Units carry painted
serial numbers; buildings carry a plate with the name of the district that raised them.

Its anthem is the depot whistle at shift change. Its slogan, which appears on the first line of every teletype
header, is plain: COUNT THE LIVING, MEND THE LINE.
