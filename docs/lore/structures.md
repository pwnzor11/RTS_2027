# Structures

One entry per rules id (28). Format: `title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, `field_note:`, body. Authority notes read as teletype;
Directorate notes as Voice broadcast. Names are working names.

## Authority

### auth_conyard
title: Construction Yard
subtitle: Base seat of an Authority district
unlock: open
field_note: DEPOT 1 // ALL BUILDING STARTS HERE. SIGN THE ROSTER. STOP.

A prefabricated depot of cranes, gantries and drawing boards, carried out in crates and assembled on arrival. Every
Authority base starts as a yard, and every yard starts with a plan pinned to the wall. The first thing built is a
flagpole, the second is a toilet, and the third is a power line.

### auth_power
title: Power Plant
subtitle: Reactor and turbine hall
unlock: open
field_note: POWER 2 // KEEP THE ROD FREE, THE FLOOR DRY, THE DIAL ABOVE ZERO. STOP.

A small sealed reactor feeding a turbine and a bank of vacuum-tube regulators. Operators sit on shifts behind a glass
window, watching needle gauges and talking to nobody. When the dial drops, the whole base notices.

### auth_refinery
title: Ashfall Refinery
subtitle: Centrifuge plant
unlock: open
field_note: REFINERY 3 // INTAKE, SPIN, SEAL. ASH IN, FUEL OUT. NO SHORTCUTS. STOP.

A concrete plant with a chimney stack and a bank of centrifuges, where scraper loads are separated into fuel concentrate.
It is the reason a base can exist, and the first thing an enemy wants to hit. Refinery crews wear sealed coveralls and
speak to each other through gesture.

### auth_barracks
title: Barracks
subtitle: Infantry muster hall
unlock: open
field_note: BARRACKS 4 // REPORT, ISSUE, DRILL. EVERY BUNK HAS A NAME. STOP.

Rows of bunks, a rifle rack, a drill yard and a decon arch at the door. The labour-year draft arrives here to be
issued boots and a number, and leaves as a company. Barracks cooks are the best-loved people on any base.

### auth_factory
title: Vehicle Factory
subtitle: Armoured vehicle works
unlock: open
field_note: FACTORY 5 // WELD, FIT, TEST. NOTHING LEAVES WITHOUT A STAMP. STOP.

A long shed of overhead cranes and assembly bays where scrapers, scout cars and tanks are built from scratch.
Everything is inspected twice, and then once more by a foreman who has been doing it since the First Winter.

### auth_silo
title: Storage Silo
subtitle: Concentrate store
unlock: open
field_note: SILO 6 // STORE DRY, STORE SEALED, COUNT TWICE. STOP.

A squat concrete bunker that holds refined concentrate in lead-lined drums. The Authority's rule, written in the
Charter, is that a silo is never filled beyond what a district can guard.

### auth_wall
title: Perimeter Wall
subtitle: Base wall segment
unlock: open
field_note: WALL 7 // POURED, NOT PAINTED. HOLD THE LINE. STOP.

A section of reinforced slab, stencilled with its district number. Walls are poured by volunteers on weekends and visited
by inspectors on Mondays. A sealed ring of them is the Authority's idea of a home.

### auth_turret
title: Gun Turret
subtitle: Fixed defensive gun
unlock: open
field_note: TURRET 8 // POWERED, TRAINED, WATCHED. DO NOT LEAVE UNMANNED. STOP.

A shielded gun on a rotating base, powered from the grid and traversed by a gunner in an armoured pit. Turrets are
named by the crews who man them, a habit the Administration discourages and cannot stop.

### auth_seismograph
title: Seismograph Station
subtitle: Underground listening post
unlock: campaign
field_note: SEISMIC 9 // THE GROUND TALKS. LISTEN FOR WHAT SHOULD NOT BE THERE. STOP.

A squat hut over a bedrock pillar, with a drum and a pen that scratches a trace on moving paper. Technicians learned to read
the trace of a burrowing machine within a season of the first hatch sightings. Their warnings are the Authority's
earliest and quietest defence.

### auth_searchlight
title: Searchlight Tower
subtitle: Anti-Blackout and night watch
unlock: campaign
field_note: LIGHT 10 // BURN THROUGH THE SMOKE. SEE FIRST, SHOOT SECOND. STOP.

A steel mast with a carbon-arc lamp and a reflector big enough to read by from a mile away. The lamp cuts through Blackout
smoke and exposes anything in the beam. The Authority's belief is simple: if you light the ground, there is less to fear.

### auth_gate
title: Gate
subtitle: Perimeter gate
unlock: campaign
field_note: GATE 11 // OPEN FOR OURS, SHUT FOR THEM. SIGN THE LOG AT EVERY PASSAGE. STOP.

A reinforced sliding gate keyed to the district's own vehicles. It opens for friendly convoys and counts as wall for the
seal. Gate-keepers are a proud profession and the most unforgiving checkers of papers on the continent.

### auth_engineering
title: Engineering Works
subtitle: Research and standards bureau
unlock: campaign
field_note: ENGINEERING 12 // DESIGN, TEST, ISSUE AS BULLETIN. NOTHING IS A SECRET. STOP.

A drafting hall and testing yard where new methods are worked out and issued as bulletins. Engineers argue about
tolerances for weeks, then release a result that everyone uses for decades.

## Directorate

### dir_shaft
title: Command Shaft
subtitle: Seat of an advance post
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "THE SHAFT IS DEEP, THE DIRECTORATE DEEPER. REPORT BELOW, CITIZEN."

A reinforced shaft head with a lift cage, an antenna mast and a heavy hatch. Above ground there is almost nothing to see;
below it, everything. The Directorate treats a shaft the way the Authority treats a flag: a place where the government
simply is.

### dir_reactor_vent
title: Reactor Vent
subtitle: Power source
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "THE WARMTH OF THE INTERIOR COMES UP THROUGH THE VENT. STAND NEAR, STAND PROUD."

A cooling stack on top of a buried reactor, pouring a shimmering haze into the cold air. The reactor is deep and unseen;
the stack is the only part that can be shot at. It keeps the whole post running and everyone nearby slightly warm.

### dir_smelter_vent
title: Smelter Vent
subtitle: Ashfall processing outlet
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "WHAT THE SURFACE LEAVES BEHIND, THE INTERIOR REFINES. NOTHING IS WASTED."

A hatch-and-chimney complex where burrowing scrapers deliver ash to the smelters below. Fumes rise from the vent day
and night, which makes it easy to find and an obvious target.

### dir_muster_hatch
title: Muster Hatch
subtitle: Conscript barracks
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "FORM UP, FORM UP, FORM UP. THE INTERIOR HAS A PLACE FOR EVERY CITIZEN."

A wide steel hatch opening onto a drill hall, where conscripts are issued rifles and a number. Their service credit is
stamped on a card and kept in a locked drawer. It is also a tunnel entrance, so reinforcements surface anywhere the
network reaches.

### dir_assembly_bore
title: Assembly Bore
subtitle: Vehicle works
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "THE INTERIOR BUILDS WHAT THE SURFACE FORGOT HOW TO BUILD."

A hangar-sized cavern entrance with a gantry and a trolley line. Vehicles are built below and rolled up a ramp. Few in number, large
in ambition, and all of them slightly strange.

### dir_hatch
title: Hatch
subtitle: Small tunnel entrance
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "A DOOR IS ONLY A DOOR UNTIL THE INTERIOR OPENS IT."

A reinforced hatch with a lift cage for infantry. Hatches are dug by Bore crews and connect the Directorate's
network. They are modest above ground and meaningful below.

### dir_bore_head
title: Bore Head
subtitle: Large tunnel entrance
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "WHEN THE SMALL DOOR IS NOT ENOUGH, THE INTERIOR OPENS A LARGER ONE."

A ramped shaft mouth that takes vehicles as well as infantry. It is the heavy artery of the network, and an
important reason the Authority fears an empty field.

### dir_cistern
title: Cistern
subtitle: Concentrate store
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "HELD IN TRUST. EVERY DROP IS COUNTED."

A buried tank with a narrow access hatch, holding refined concentrate. There is little to see on the surface and
less to take.

### dir_bunker
title: Bunker Wall
subtitle: Defensive wall piece
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "A WALL IS A PROMISE. THE INTERIOR KEEPS ITS PROMISES."

A low bone-white slab, set into the ground with a red stripe along the top. Cheap to raise, it is the Directorate's
way of saying that the ground behind it is spoken for.

### dir_pillbox
title: Pillbox
subtitle: Small defensive gun
unlock: open
field_note: VOICE: "SEE THE SLIT, CITIZEN? THAT IS THE INTERIOR WATCHING."

A squat concrete turret with a narrow firing slit and a loudspeaker. It is manned by a pair of conscripts who are
told never to open the door, and are not allowed to forget it.

### dir_rocket_nest
title: Rocket Nest
subtitle: Anti-aircraft battery
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE SKY IS ALSO THE INTERIOR'S. LET NO ONE FORGET IT."

A sandbagged pit with a rack of rockets pointed at the sky. It is a reply to the one thing the Directorate does not
have enough of, and a reminder that it too once looked up in fear.

### dir_rad_mortar
title: Radiation Mortar
subtitle: Area denial battery
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "WHAT THE INTERIOR THROWS, THE GROUND KEEPS. LET THE TRESPASSER COUNT THE COST."

A buried mortar tube lobbing shells of irradiated slurry, poisoning the ground where they land. Authority engineers hate it
for the clean-up bill, and Directorate crews love it for the same reason.

### dir_mast
title: Broadcast Mast
subtitle: Radar and Static Broadcast tower
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "LISTEN. THE INTERIOR SPEAKS ON EVERY BAND, AND NO OTHER VOICE IS NEEDED."

A tall lattice mast with a rotating dish and a ring of horns. It watches the sky and drowns enemy radio and maps in
static. The Voice's lullaby plays from it at lights-out.

### dir_isotope_works
title: Isotope Works
subtitle: Research and weapons laboratory
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE LEARNED CITIZENS OF THE INTERIOR SERVE THE FUTURE. BE PATIENT WITH THEM."

A shielded laboratory with a vent and a bank of glowing tubes, the domain of Dr. Lund's staff. Research here is real
and dangerous, and almost every new weapon in the Directorate's arsenal began on one of its benches.

### dir_mooring
title: Mooring Tower
subtitle: Dirigible dock
unlock: campaign
field_note: VOICE: "THE INTERIOR RISES. WATCH IT RISE."

A steel mooring mast and a hangar at its base, where dirigibles are inflated, serviced and launched. It is the most
cheerful building the Directorate owns, and the staff know it.

### dir_sunrise
title: The Sunrise Device
subtitle: Buried warhead
unlock: encounter
field_note: VOICE: "AND AT LAST THE DAWN. ALL CITIZENS SHALL SEE IT."

A deep chamber with a surface vent and a launch cradle, built around a recovered warhead that is lowered by tunnel to
beneath a chosen target. The Directorate calls it the Sunrise and treats it as holy. The Authority calls it a
second Exchange in the making.
