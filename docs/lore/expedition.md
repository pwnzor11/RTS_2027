# The Overseas Expedition

Status: approved by the owner 2026-11-04 (Q-38)

Entry ids use the prefix `faction_exp_`, `leader_exp_` and `exp_`. Format per entry: `title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, `field_note:`
(units and structures), body. Expedition field notes read as typed telex from the fleet. Every leader and institution
is invented; the old continent is never named and no present-day government appears. Names are working names (design 07).

## The faction

### faction_exp_origin
title: The Far Shore
subtitle: Where the Expedition comes from
unlock: open

Across the ocean there is another continent that also burned in October 1962, and that burned a little less. Its
cities were struck, but its mountain governments held their doors open a few hours longer, and its prevailing winds
carried the ash out to sea instead of down onto its fields. The survivors there rebuilt around ports, shipyards and
the research institutes that had been dug into the rock.

They call what remains the Tidewater Concordat: a council of surviving ministries, each with its own office, seal and
quarrel. The Concordat is old, tired and very good at engineering. It still builds sealed-core ships, hover skiffs and
jet aircraft, because it never stopped teaching the people who know how.

### faction_exp_reason
title: Why They Came
subtitle: The Second Relief Fleet
unlock: open

For thirty years the Concordat believed no one was left on the far side of the ocean. In 1996 a faint carrier signal
from the north-west reached a listening post, and a Hydrographic Office team traced it to a continent that still had
reactors burning on its coasts. A first relief fleet sailed in 1997 and was never heard from again.

The Second Relief Fleet sailed in the spring of 2000: forty-one ships, a hundred aircraft and eleven thousand people
who volunteered. The official reason was rescue. The unofficial reason, which every officer knows, is fuel. The
Concordat's own fissile stocks are running down, and the richest ash fields on earth lie across the water.

### faction_exp_government
title: The Concordat of Surviving Ministries
subtitle: How the Expedition is governed
unlock: open

The Expedition has no throne and no party. It is governed by the same ministries that sent it: the Ministry of
Passage, which owns the ships; the Hydrographic Office, which owns the charts and the sonar; the Institute of Applied
Valve Physics at Carrow Down, which owns the technology; and the Office of Return, which owns the purse and the
patience. The fleet answers to all four and takes orders from none of them in a hurry.

At sea the fleet is run by its Admiral. Ashore the Commissioner for the Return speaks for the home government. When
the two disagree, as they usually do, a telex goes home and a month passes.

### faction_exp_culture
title: A Navy That Remembers Peace
subtitle: Culture of the Expedition
unlock: campaign

Expedition crews are the children of harbours. They are proud of clean decks, hand-painted hull numbers and a
handshake that outlasts a signature. They have seen sunlight in their lifetimes, and a field of ash looks to them like
a sickness rather than a home. Their jokes are about weather and wages, and their prayers are mostly about parts.

They are also fewer than they look. Every crew member has a name on a roster in a port they may never see again, and
every lost hull is a month of home production. The fleet fights carefully, expensively and well.

### faction_exp_symbols
title: Marks and Colours
subtitle: Symbols of the Expedition
unlock: open

The mark of the fleet is an anchor crossed with a compass needle inside a ring of eight stars, one for each founding
ministry. Its colours are deep navy, lagoon white and polished copper, with signal orange for anything that lands or
lifts. Hulls carry a number, a name and a thin ribbon of the home port's colour that no one has seen in years.

Its signal flag is a white pennant. Its slogan, the opening line of every fleet telex, is: RETURN WITH THE TIDE.

## Leaders

### leader_exp_aldersey
title: Fleet Admiral Corvin Aldersey
subtitle: Commander of the Second Relief Fleet
unlock: open

A career convoy officer, Aldersey speaks to the commander over the fleet circuit in unhurried, formal sentences. He
learned his craft escorting grain ships through winters nobody else wanted to sail, and he treats every sailor as an
irreplaceable tool. He has never won a decisive battle and has never lost a convoy.

Motivation: bring as many ships home as he took out, and turn the fleet's one-way ticket into a round trip.

Secret: He has read the signal log of the first relief fleet. It did not vanish; it was last heard asking for rescue
from the south coast, and the Concordat chose not to send help. Aldersey follows his orders and keeps the log in his
safe.

### leader_exp_strand
title: Commissioner Imelda Strand
subtitle: Commissioner for the Return
unlock: open

Strand is the Office of Return's voice ashore: a former harbour auditor who counts tonnage, ration and goodwill in
the same ledger. She sets the commander's objectives by telex, always in the form of a polite request, and never
describes a landing as a conquest. She is the reason the fleet pays its bills.

Motivation: make the expedition worth what it cost, so that the Concordat will send another.

Secret: There will not be another. The Concordat's council is fracturing at home, and Strand has received three
unsent orders to recall the fleet. She has quietly kept the shipments flowing with her own signature.

### leader_exp_roque
title: Dr. Anselm Roque
subtitle: Chief Engineer of the Expedition
unlock: open

Roque runs the fleet's shipboard institute out of a converted cargo hold. He is a gentle, distracted man who talks to
valves and apologises to machines. Everything that makes the fleet advanced was drawn on his drafting tables.

Motivation: keep the fleet's technology alive long enough for the Concordat to relearn how to build it.

Secret: The sealed cores degrade faster than the specifications admit. Without spare parts that only the home
institute can make, most of the fleet will be scrap within three years, and Roque knows which hulls will fail first.

### leader_exp_kell
title: Air Marshal Perpetua Kell
subtitle: Commander of the fleet air arm
unlock: campaign

Kell flew rescue sorties in the last hard winters at home and never lost the habit of counting heads on the way back.
She runs the carrier decks like a ward, loudly and with affection, and treats every sortie as a promise.

Motivation: land every airman where they took off. She is willing to lose a campaign to keep that promise.

Secret: She has already taken personal command of a rescue flight into the Glass Coast against orders, and found
people living there who were never on any manifest.

## Units

### exp_marine
title: Marine
subtitle: Amphibious infantry
unlock: open
field_note: LANDING SECTION 1 // WADE, SECURE, HOLD THE BEACH. KEEP THE RADIO DRY. TELEX ENDS.

A volunteer from the ports, issued a sealed rebreather, a gas-operated rifle and one of the few helmets that fits. The
Marine is not many, and he knows his name is on the roster at home. He goes ashore first and is expected to be the last
to leave.

### exp_pathfinder
title: Pathfinder
subtitle: Spotter and designator
unlock: campaign
field_note: SPOTTER 3 // LASE THE TARGET, CALL THE FALL OF SHOT, THEN MOVE. TELEX ENDS.

A Hydrographic Office surveyor trained to read a shoreline from a mile off. With a stabilised range finder and a
shoulder radio, a Pathfinder turns an ordinary hillside into a firing table for the guns offshore.

### exp_skimmer
title: Hover Skimmer
subtitle: Amphibious harvester
unlock: open
field_note: SKIMMER 2 // LIGHT, FAST, AND EXPENSIVE. DO NOT BEACH IT ON SHARP ROCK. TELEX ENDS.

A skirted hovercraft with a suction scoop and a lead-lined hopper, built to skim ash from shallows no tracked scraper
can reach. It rides on a cushion of air and a great deal of anxiety.

### exp_glide_tank
title: Glide Tank
subtitle: Hover medium armour
unlock: open
field_note: GLIDE 4 // FAST, GUIDED, LIGHTLY ARMOURED. FIGHT AT RANGE, DISENGAGE EARLY. TELEX ENDS.

A skirted hover tank with a stabilised guided cannon, designed at Carrow Down for crossing the water between beaches and
fields without stopping. It will outpace any tracked tank on the continent and lose a straight fight with one.

### exp_landing_craft
title: Landing Craft
subtitle: Beachable transport
unlock: open
field_note: LANDER 7 // EIGHT SOULS OR TWO VEHICLES. DROP THE RAMP, THEN GO BACK FOR MORE. TELEX ENDS.

A blunt, shallow-draft ship with a bow ramp and an armoured crew compartment. Its bow wears the names of the ports the
crew expects never to see again.

### exp_destroyer
title: Destroyer
subtitle: Escort warship
unlock: campaign
field_note: ESCORT 12 // DEPTH CHARGE AND GUN. KEEP THE CONVOY BETWEEN YOU AND THE WEATHER. TELEX ENDS.

A turbine destroyer with a sealed core and a bridge full of radar scopes. Its crew spent years guarding grain convoys in
winter seas and take it personally when a hull goes under.

### exp_missile_sub
title: Missile Submarine
subtitle: Silent striker
unlock: campaign
field_note: SILENT 2 // SURFACE ONLY TO FIRE. DO NOT BE FOUND. TELEX ENDS.

A sealed-core boat with six guided missiles and a crew that has not spoken above a murmur in two years. It can strike
a shoreline from beyond sight and is hunted for exactly that reason.

### exp_cruiser
title: Cruiser
subtitle: Bombardment ship
unlock: campaign
field_note: HEAVY 1 // LONG GUNS, LONG MEMORY. WAIT FOR THE SPOTTER. TELEX ENDS.

A nuclear-powered gun cruiser, the largest vessel the Concordat could afford to risk. Its turrets can throw a shell far
inland, but only at what a Pathfinder or a gyro can see for it.

### exp_carrier
title: Carrier
subtitle: Floating airbase
unlock: campaign
field_note: DECK 1 // FOUR SORTIES, FOUR HOMECOMINGS. COUNT THEM IN AND COUNT THEM OUT. TELEX ENDS.

The flagship of the air arm: a slab of flight deck over a sealed reactor, with a hangar bay that smells of oil and tea.
Air Marshal Kell holds that a carrier is the only place on the continent where every lost airman is counted twice.

### exp_ironbound
title: Ironbound Trooper
subtitle: Powered-suit heavy infantry
unlock: campaign
field_note: HEAVY LANDING 9 // SLOW, ARMOURED, NOT EXPENDABLE. DO NOT LEAVE IT BEHIND. TELEX ENDS.

A volunteer in a valve-driven powered suit, with shoulder-mounted anti-air missiles and hydraulic gauntlets. There are
only a few suits, and each carries the name of the engineer who built it stencilled inside the helmet.

## Structures

### exp_landing_depot
title: Landing Depot
subtitle: Expedition construction yard
unlock: open
field_note: DEPOT 1 // UNPACK THE COMMAND SHIP. STAKE THE BEACH. HANG THE FLAG. TELEX ENDS.

When the Command Ship reaches the beach her hull folds open and becomes a base: cranes, a telex room, and a flagpole that
is raised before the first wall. The depot is only as safe as the beaches it can hold.

### exp_beacon
title: Landing Beacon
subtitle: Forward beachhead marker
unlock: open
field_note: BEACON 2 // LIGHT THE LAMP, CLAIM THE SHORE. IT WILL NOT SURVIVE A NIGHT ALONE. TELEX ENDS.

A tripod lamp, an aerial and a tent of sealed crates, set down by landing craft or airlift. Wherever it burns the fleet
can raise buildings, and wherever it goes dark the fleet must carry its own tools.

### exp_anchorage
title: Anchorage
subtitle: Shipment quay
unlock: open
field_note: QUAY 4 // THE FLEET'S ONE LINE HOME. KEEP IT OPEN. TELEX ENDS.

A concrete pier with a tide gauge, a crane and a telex mast. Every few minutes a convoy of supply tenders arrives with
parts, rations and the occasional letter. The Commissioner reads the shipment manifest before the Admiral does.

### exp_core_hall
title: Core Hall
subtitle: Valve-computer research hall
unlock: campaign
field_note: HALL 3 // THE FLEET'S ONLY MEMORY. DO NOT SWITCH OFF THE COOLING. TELEX ENDS.

A long room of glass-fronted racks and humming valve banks, kept at the temperature of a cellar. Dr. Roque sleeps here
more than he admits, and the staff have learned to walk quietly past his cot.

### exp_salvo_array
title: Salvo Array
subtitle: Fleet missile barrage
unlock: campaign
field_note: SALVO 1 // TWELVE MISSILES, ONE AIM POINT. IT CANNOT BE RECALLED. TELEX ENDS.

A pier of launch rails and control gantries that links the fleet's missile ships into a single barrage. The Concordat
built it as a deterrent for a war that never came to its own coast.
