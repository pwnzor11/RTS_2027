### auth_grouting
title: Foundation Grouting
subtitle: Hardened ground inside the walls
unlock: campaign
field_note: ENGINEERING BULLETIN 11 // POUR, SET, CURE. IF THE WALL HOLDS, THE FLOOR HOLDS. STOP.

Researched at the Engineering Works, Foundation Grouting fills the soft ground enclosed by a base's walls, gates and buildings with
quick-curing concrete slurry. Nothing can surface or break through there. The Authority thinks of it as the civil engineer's reply
to tunnelling: pour the floor and be done with it. A single broken wall panel spoils the seal, which is why work crews
inspect the perimeter every morning.
