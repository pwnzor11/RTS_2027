### faction_dir_bunker
title: Forty Years Below
subtitle: Bunker culture of the Directorate
unlock: open

The Annex was built for eleven hundred people and has held up to eleven thousand. It runs on shifts, on rank, and on the
rule that no one may be seen idle. Rooms are numbered by depth; a person's status is how many levels beneath the first
blast door they sleep. Light is rationed, so the Directorate learned to work by dim red lamps and to keep the old
habit of whispering. Bone-white concrete, red signal paint, brass speaker grilles in every corridor.

Children born below have never seen a sky. They learn that the surface is not gone but only unreclaimed, an unpaid
debt held in trust. This is not a story they are told to believe. It is the only world they have, and the Directorate
has made it work.

### faction_dir_ideology
title: The Doctrine of Reclamation
subtitle: Why the Directorate believes it must surface
unlock: campaign

From inside, the logic is airtight. The Director held the government of the whole continent in trust, therefore the
government is the Annex, and the people above are runaway tenants. The surface is not an enemy but unfinished business.
Ash is not poison but the debt left by those who failed to hold the line. Every conscript below pays down that debt with
a service year; every acre reclaimed is a payment received.

There is also a quieter reason. The Annex is failing. Reactors age, air plants clog, and the old shafts cannot hold
another generation. The Directorate cannot say this aloud, so it says "Reclamation" instead, with great confidence and
at volume.

### faction_dir_voice
title: The Voice
subtitle: Broadcast and propaganda
unlock: campaign

Every corridor, shaft and surface hatch has a speaker, and every speaker carries the Voice: warm, measured, never
hurried. It reads the Director's orders, the shift lists, the weather (always grim) and the lullaby at lights-out. It
calls conscripts "citizens in service", the Authority "the tenancy", and a defeat "a redistribution of effort".

Broadcast is also a weapon. Loudspeaker crawlers carry the Voice into the field, where it steadies the faithful and
drowns the doubtful. Nobody inside has heard the Director speak live in thirty years, but nobody doubts the Voice
speaks for him. That is rather the point.

### faction_dir_surfacing
title: Why the Directorate Came Up
subtitle: The surfacing, 1985 to 2001
unlock: campaign

The Annex held on for two generations by trimming its numbers and tightening its rules. In 1994, when Shaft Seven flooded
and its reactor failed, the margin vanished. The Commissariat of Order, which had always policed the shifts, took over
planning, and its plan was simple: go up, take the fields, take the launch sites, take the world back to the Director.

The Directorate does not think of this as invasion. It thinks of it as the end of a forty-year emergency. Its engineers
bore under the Rust Belt, its conscripts surface in the ash, and its loudspeakers announce that the Interior has returned.
Many who are sent up believe every word. A few begin to notice the sky.
