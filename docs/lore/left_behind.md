# The Left Behind

Status: approved by the owner 2026-11-04 (Q-38)

Entry ids use the prefix `faction_lb_` for faction pages and `lb_` for unit and structure pages. Format per entry:
`title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, optional `field_note:`, body. Leader names are invented. Nothing here contradicts
design 03 "Decided" or the canon in D-086 (Brigadier Wrenfield's brother was left behind on the Glass Coast in 1990).
Field notes read as caravan chalk-board messages and rig-radio chatter.

## Timeline 1990-2001 (for the Archive overview page)

- **Winter 1990.** The Great Drift buries the Glass Coast. The Authority orders an evacuation by convoy; the roads
  close under the ash before the last trains leave.
- **Spring 1990.** About four thousand people remain at the Tern Point railhead. The final convoy does not return.
- **1990-91.** The railhead families strip the stranded trains and build the first crawler-rigs.
- **1994.** The first caravan crosses the Drift to the dune sea. They are known to nobody and trade with no one.
- **1997.** Rig-masters hold the first Gathering at Kettle Flat. They choose a Speaker.
- **2001.** The Authority and Directorate meet openly. The caravans notice that both are looking at the Glass Coast.

### faction_lb_abandonment
title: The Last Train
subtitle: The abandonment, 1990
unlock: open

In the winter of 1990 the ash came down so thickly that it buried the coast road in a night. The Authority's convoy
masters called the evacuation; the trains were loaded in order of rank, then of clerkship, then of luck. When the
ash closed the road, there were four thousand people left at the Tern Point railhead with eleven working trucks and a
telegraph line that went dead on the third morning.

There was no letter, no official regret, and no search party. The people at Tern Point do not say they were
betrayed. They say they were counted, and that the count stopped. They kept the list they had made while they waited,
and every child of the camp is named on it.

### faction_lb_first_rigs
title: The First Rigs
subtitle: How the camp learned to move, 1990 to 1994
unlock: open

The stranded trains became the first homes. A boiler car was a kitchen; a flatbed was a market; a locomotive with its
reactor core gone cold was a workshop and a warning. By the second winter someone had bolted a cab and a pair of
tracks to a flatbed and driven it ten metres without it falling over. By the fourth, the whole camp could move.

The first caravan left Tern Point in 1994. The railhead was not abandoned a second time: the rigs were built so that
nothing, ever again, had to be left behind. Every rig carries a flag with the names of the people on it.

### faction_lb_culture
title: Caravan Custom
subtitle: How the Left Behind live
unlock: open

A rig is a family, a trade and a town. Rigs are named for what they carry or what they lost: *Second Chance*, *Kettle*,
*Nobody's Daughter*. Status is how long a rig has stayed afloat and how many strangers it has fed. Work is shared by
skill, not rank. Disputes are settled at the Gathering, which is held wherever the wind is kindest.

The caravans keep three habits with great discipline: they bury nobody they have not salvaged from, they never leave
a wreck without looking inside it, and they never say "goodbye" to someone leaving a rig, only "see you at the next
fire". Clothes are layered cloth over sheet metal; goggles are family heirlooms.

### faction_lb_leaders
title: The Speakers
subtitle: Leaders of the caravans
unlock: campaign

The caravans have no president. At each Gathering they choose a **Speaker**, who carries messages between rigs and
holds the ledger of debts. The present Speaker is **Marrow Tindall**, a former railhead telegraphist who still taps
her thumb in code when she thinks. She is patient, dry and exact, and she has not lost an argument she cared about.

Her war-captain is **Jessup Calloway-Ross**, the rig-master of *Kettle* and the best driver on the Drift. He is loud
where she is quiet, and the two have not agreed on anything in six years except that the Authority should be asked
first. The third voice is **Old Ottoline Vane**, the oldest woman on the Drift, who remembers the last train and has never
told anyone what she saw from the platform.

### faction_lb_authority
title: What the Left Behind Think of the Authority
subtitle: A long memory
unlock: campaign

The caravans do not hate the Authority; they simply remember the exact day the lists stopped. Some think the order to close the
road was a mistake. Others think it was arithmetic. Marrow Tindall holds that it makes no difference to the people on the
platform. When Authority survey cars appear on the dunes, the rigs offer water and a fair price for fuel, and do not offer a name.

An Authority officer, Brigadier Wrenfield, is known to the Drift. His brother was among those on the platform in 1990 and has
not been seen since; the caravans have a name for him, and the Brigadier has not yet asked for it.

### faction_lb_directorate
title: What the Left Behind Think of the Directorate
subtitle: Strangers with loudspeakers
unlock: campaign

The Directorate is a rumour that arrived as a voice. The first Directorate broadcast the caravans heard told them to
register for service. The caravans' reply was to retune the loudspeaker on the *Kettle* and play it back with a slightly
different wording. They do not fear the Voice, but they do not like what it does to the dunes at night.

### lb_scavenger
title: Scavenger
subtitle: Caravan rifleman
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SCAV CREW, FIVE ABOARD. BRING BACK THE PIPE, LEAVE THE BODY. WE KEEP LISTS.

Every child on the Drift is taught to pick through a wreck before they are taught to read. A Scavenger carries a scrap
rifle, a coil of rope and a spare filter tin. They are cheerful, grimy and very hard to surprise, and they treat every
fight as a chance to restock.

### lb_hook_runner
title: Hook Runner
subtitle: Raider on a line
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // HOOK ONE. IN AND OUT BEFORE THE ALARM FINISHES. DON'T TELL MA.

The best climbers on the Drift. A Hook Runner throws a weighted line over a wall or a silo rim, goes up, takes what
he can carry and drops back down before the first shot. The caravans call them "the quick hands", and every
rig keeps a bed for one.

### lb_slinger
title: Slinger
subtitle: Shaped-charge thrower
unlock: campaign
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SLING TEAM. TWO CHARGES EACH, EVERY CHARGE NAMED. WASTE ONE AND YOU PAINT THE NEXT.

Armed with a sling-arm and a bagful of hand-packed charges cast in tin cans, the Slinger answers armour the way the
caravans answer everything: with whatever is left over. They are brave in a quiet way and rarely stand still.

### lb_tinker
title: Tinker
subtitle: Field mechanic
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // TINKER ON DECK. KEEP HER ALIVE, SHE KEEPS EVERYTHING ELSE ALIVE.

Every rig has a Tinker, and every Tinker has a bag of odd tools. They patch plates, rewire valves, and bargain over the
price of a tube. The Left Behind will follow a Tinker into a fight they would otherwise avoid, because they know who
is going to fix them afterwards.

### lb_veilwalker
title: Veilwalker
subtitle: Dust scout
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // WALKER LOST IN THE GREY. SHE KNOWS WHERE SHE IS. WE DON'T. THAT IS THE POINT.

The Veilwalkers move in the ash, wrapped in sail-cloth the colour of the drifts. They are rarely seen, never
photographed, and are reported by the Authority as "environmental interference". The caravans simply call them the
quiet ones.

### lb_gleaner
title: Gleaner
subtitle: Ash harvester
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // GLEANER 3. HOPPER IS HALF FULL AND THE WIND IS TURNING. COME HOME.

A scrap-built scoop on a bus chassis. The Gleaner is slower, lighter and less respected than the Authority's Scraper,
and its drivers pay for it by being very good at running. A well-run caravan keeps three at the field and one at the
camp for spare parts.

### lb_harpoon_wagon
title: Harpoon Wagon
subtitle: Tethering anti-armour
unlock: campaign
field_note: CHALK BOARD // HARPOON TWO. HOLD HER STEADY. WE ARE NOT GOING TO PULL HIM OFF HIS TRACKS. WE ARE GOING TO TURN HIM.

A flatbed fitted with an old whaling gun and a drum of cable, bought from a coastal wreck and rebuilt a dozen times.
The harpoon hooks a hull and drags it off course. Enemy crews hate it most for the noise.

### lb_rolling_fort
title: Rolling Fort
subtitle: The caravans' heavy
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // FORT ON THE RIDGE. EVERYONE LOOK BUSY. LET THEM COME TO US.

Built from three wrecked buses, a bridge girder and an engine nobody can identify, the Fort is the biggest thing the
caravans can make that still moves. It is not fast and it is not pretty. It is, however, almost impossible to bring down
quickly, because every plate is a different age and every weak point is somewhere new.

### lb_mother_rig
title: Mother Rig
subtitle: The caravan's home and workshop
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // MOTHER RIG UP. THE KETTLE IS ON. ALL RIGS REPORT.

The oldest rig in any caravan and the one that never gets rebuilt in the same shape twice. A Mother Rig packs down to a
single flatbed with four hundred names on its flag, and unpacks into a street. Everything that matters on the Drift
starts here, including the arguments.

### lb_second_drift
title: The Second Drift
subtitle: Superweapon
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // SPEAKER TO ALL RIGS. THE WIND IS OURS TODAY. KEEP YOUR MASKS ON.

The caravans do not have a bomb. What they have is thirty years of knowing how the ash moves. Using stolen anemometers,
a salvaged rocket pod and the memory of the winter that buried the coast, a rig-master can shape a storm that
buries a battlefield. It is the closest the Left Behind come to revenge, and the Speaker has never been seen to
smile when it is used.

### lb_drifter_brute
title: Drifter Brute
subtitle: Dust-tank infantry
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // BRUTE ON THE LINE. HE IS NOT A SOLDIER. HE IS A WALL THAT WALKS. STAY BEHIND HIM.

A big figure in welded plate, carrying a flame-hammer on a tank of dust-oil strapped to the back. The Drifter Brute walks into the enemy line and burns a path through it, then keeps walking. They handle armour badly and know it, so they are kept on the flanks where the fire is thickest. Veterans say a good Brute is worth a squad of anyone else in the open, and a bad one is always the last thing you see.

### lb_hauler
title: Salvage Hauler
subtitle: Wreck collector
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // HAULER ON THE ROAD. TWO WRECKS IN THE BED, ONE MORE BEHIND. DON'T WAIT FOR US.

A long-bed truck with a winch at the back and a cage of hooks along the sides. The Hauler goes after the wrecks the Gleaners cannot reach and drags them home, one dead car at a time. Each rig counts its salvage by the weight in the bed, and the drivers keep the tally on the tailgate in chalk. Most of the Drift's spare parts have passed through one.

### lb_buggy
title: Dune Buggy
subtitle: Fast scout
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // BUGGY OUT ON THE FLATS. SHE HAS SEEN THE SMOKE. SHE IS NOT STOPPING TO COUNT IT.

An open frame on four salvaged wheels, a light gun on a swivel and a driver who never looks comfortable. The buggy runs ahead of the rigs to find water, wrecks and trouble, then reports back by flag or radio. It is too light to stand a fight for long. The caravans send it anyway, because it is the only thing on the Drift that can outrun a patrol and still come home.

### lb_organ
title: Organ
subtitle: Rocket-rack artillery
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // ORGAN READY. AIM IS A RUMOUR. AIM AT THE BIG ONES AND HOPE.

Eight rocket tubes bolted to a flatbed, each one a different pattern, fired in a rolling volley. The Organ is never accurate and everyone who fires it knows it. What it lacks in aim it makes up in noise and area, and a column of Authority crawlers under an Organ volley will scatter. The gunners call the rack the choir and tune its tubes by ear before every run.

### lb_tow
title: Tow Truck
subtitle: Transport and rig-tow
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // TOW IS HOOKED ON. SIX ABOARD AND ONE SULKING MOTHER RIG. HOLD THE LINE.

A heavy bus chassis with a rear troop bay for six and a tow-arm that lifts a packed rig onto its bed. The Tow Truck is the caravan's ferry: it carries the families who cannot walk, the spares that cannot be lost, and the rigs that must move in a hurry. Its drivers are the only people on the Drift trusted with everyone's children at once, and they say so with a straight face.

### lb_dust_sail
title: Dust Sail
subtitle: Wind-rigged screen
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SAIL UP ON THE RIDGE. WIND IS OURS. KEEP THE GREY BETWEEN US AND THEM.

A great sheet of sail-cloth stretched over a frame and mounted on a flatbed, angled to the wind. When the ash runs thick, the Dust Sail raises a grey wall that blinds the enemy's sight. It cannot shoot, and it cannot protect a crew from artillery. It only makes the field harder to see across, which is usually enough for the Veilwalkers to do their work.

### lb_rotor
title: Rotor
subtitle: Autogyro gunship
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // ROTOR UP. LOW AND SLOW. IF YOU HEAR THE HUM, GET UNDER A ROOF.

A two-seat autogyro with a machine gun in a pod and a rotor made from three wind-turbine blades. It flies low over the dunes, where the pilots can see the ground and where the flak is least likely to reach them. The Rotor hunts infantry and harvesters across the flats. Its pilots say the Authority never learned to fly, which is not quite true, but they enjoy saying it.

### lb_kite
title: Kite
subtitle: One-use glider bomber
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // KITE FLYING. ONE WAY. TELL THE KIDS IT WAS A FINE FLIGHT.

A glider of sail-cloth and a salvaged fuselage, loaded with a charge and released from the top of a ridge. It cannot come home. The Kite is cheap to build and simple to fly, which makes it the caravan's answer to big targets it cannot otherwise reach. Its pilots are volunteers, and the Speaker has never refused one. Each pilot is named on the flag of the rig that flew them.

### lb_mother_rig_packed
title: Packed Rig
subtitle: A rig in transit
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // PACKING. DON'T TOUCH THE FLAG. IT IS HALF FULL AND THEY ARE ALL IN IT.

Any rig folded for the road: a single flatbed with its walls, towers and workshops folded into its own frame and lashed under a tarpaulin. A packed rig cannot fire and cannot repair. It moves quickly and hides in any convoy, which is why raiders go for it first. Unpacking one takes most of an hour, and the families inside spend that hour arguing about where the stove should go.

### lb_yard
title: Salvage Yard
subtitle: Fixed refinery and silo
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // YARD OPEN. SCRAP IN THE NORTH GATE, PIPE IN THE SOUTH. NO BODIES IN THE HOPPER.

A walled lot of rusted sheds, a squat smelter and a silo that never gets emptied. The Salvage Yard does the work of a refinery without moving an inch, which suits a camp that stays put for a season. It is cheap to raise and cheap to lose, and the families who run it are the ones who remember every wreck that ever came in through its gate.

### lb_refinery_rig
title: Refinery Rig
subtitle: Mobile ash refinery
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // REFINERY ROLLING. HOLD TANK FULL. DON'T LET THE FIRE GO OUT.

A rig that carries its own furnace, hopper and filter bank on one wide chassis. Ash and scrap go in at the back, and clean feedstock comes out of a spout at the front, ready for the workshop. The Refinery Rig packs down for travel like everything else the caravans own. That is useful, because the Drift does not keep anything in one place for long.

### lb_dynamo_rig
title: Dynamo Rig
subtitle: Cheap small power
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // DYNAMO HUMMING. LIGHTS ON IN THE KITCHEN. DON'T ASK WHERE THE BELT CAME FROM.

A generator built from a bus engine, a line of belts and a flywheel the size of a cartwheel. It lights the cook tent, charges the radios and hums through the night. The Dynamo Rig is loud, smelly and never quite trusted, but no camp on the Drift has ever gone dark while one was running. Its operators keep a spare belt in every pocket.

field_note: RIG RADIO // STILL RUNNING. DRINK FROM THE LEFT TAP ONLY.

A copper still, a condenser coil and a rain-catch awning bolted to one wide chassis. It turns brackish runoff into water a caravan can drink, and a camp forms around it within the hour. The Still Rig packs down like every rig the caravans own, and the people follow it.

### lb_muster_rig
title: Muster Rig
subtitle: Infantry production
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // MUSTER CALL. EVERY HAND ON DECK, EVERY NAME ON THE LIST. NOBODY IS COUNTED OUT.

A rig with a canteen, a barracks car and a board where the names of new recruits are chalked up. Every caravan fighter starts here, usually as a child who was told to carry water and kept carrying it. The Muster Rig is where the Left Behind take in strangers, feed them and give them a place on the flag. Recruits are not drafted. They are asked, and most say yes.

### lb_garage_rig
title: Garage Rig
subtitle: Vehicle workshop
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // GARAGE OPEN. BRING ME YOUR BROKEN THINGS AND I WILL MAKE THEM WORSE.

A rig that unfolds into a bay with hydraulic lifts, a forge and a row of hanging chains. Every vehicle the caravan builds is finished here, and every vehicle it has lost is brought here to be patched. The Garage Rig is the reason the Left Behind can field a Rolling Fort at all. Its crew are proud of their mess, and they will tell you the gear that works best is the gear that looks worst.

### lb_lookout
title: Lookout Mast
subtitle: Radar and jamming
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // MAST UP. WE CAN SEE THEM. THEY CANNOT SEE US. THAT IS THE WHOLE TRICK.

A tall lattice mast with a dish, a receiver and a bank of noise generators at its foot. The Lookout Mast listens for the Authority's survey signals and drowns them in static, so the caravan sees the enemy on its own screen while the enemy sees only grey. It is the caravan's eyes, and the first thing a raider tries to pull down. Its operators are called listeners, and every rig respects them.

### lb_bench
title: Tinker's Bench
subtitle: Workshop and research
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // BENCH LIT. ANYTHING YOU WANT TO KNOW IS ON THE BOARD BY MORNING.

A long workbench under a tin roof, lined with jars of valves, coils and salvaged gauges. It is where the Tinkers test the ideas every rig will later use: the plate that rattles less, the pack that folds faster, the hook that holds. Nothing new reaches the Drift without passing this bench. The Speaker's rule is that a good idea is only good once it has been bent and broken here at least twice.

### lb_rotor_pad
title: Rotor Pad
subtitle: Autogyro landing and fuel
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // PAD CLEAR. WIND FROM THE NORTH. WHO LEFT THE FUEL CAN ON THE PAD?

A flat steel platform on a rig's roof, with a fuel tank, a tie-down cage and a windsock cut from an old banner. The Rotor Pad lets the caravan's autogyros take off and land without leaving the moving camp. Pilots rotate between pad and field, and the crew who keep the pad clean are the first to be blamed when the fuel runs short.

### lb_spike_nest
title: Spike Nest
subtitle: Basic defence
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // NEST FORMED. SPIKES IN THE SAND, SPIKES IN THE WALL. THEY CAN WALK INTO THEM OR GO AROUND.

A ring of sharpened rebar set in a mound of packed ash, with a lookout slot in the middle and a chain of cans to warn of approach. It costs almost nothing and stops a crew being run down on the flats. The Spike Nest is the first thing a new camp builds, usually before the first wall. ### lb_harpoon_post
title: Harpoon Post
subtitle: Anti-armour emplacement
unlock: encounter
field_note: RIG RADIO // POST ARMED. ONE HARPOON, ONE CABLE, ONE CHANCE. DON'T WASTE THE CABLE.

A fixed whaling gun on a concrete post, with a drum of steel cable beside it. The Harpoon Post is the static cousin of the Harpoon Wagon: it hooks a passing hull and drags it sideways into the ash, where the crew can finish it. It is slow to reload and very hard to move, which the caravans count as its best feature. Gunners carry a knife for the moment the cable snaps.

### lb_flak_net
title: Flak Net
subtitle: Anti-air emplacement
unlock: encounter
field_note: CHALK BOARD // NET UP. LET THEM FLY INTO THE WEB. THE WEB DOES NOT CARE WHICH SIDE THEY ARE ON.

A cluster of light guns strung across a net of cable and tin cans, which rings like a bell when anything brushes it. The Flak Net is a trap for aircraft as much as a gun. Its crew sets it at the low passes where the Rotors come in, and the noise alone turns many pilots away. The net needs two people to string and one to listen, and the caravans teach the listener first.

### lb_scrap_wall
title: Scrap Wall
subtitle: Cheap barrier
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // WALL UP. WHO PUT THE DOOR ON THE WRONG SIDE? NOT ME.

A run of bolted panels, bus doors, fence wire and bricks, stacked high enough to stop a truck. The Scrap Wall costs almost nothing and is easy to repair from what lies around the camp. It will not hold long against heavy fire, but it does not need to. Every rig keeps spare panels, because the wall is always the first thing rebuilt after a raid.

### lb_up_scrap_plate
title: Scrap Plate
subtitle: Armour from the wreck yard
unlock: research
field_note: CHALK BOARD // PLATE ON. IT RATTLES. IT STAYS ON.

Rolled sheets from a dozen wrecks, each a different thickness, welded over the vehicle's weak points. The plate adds to the armour of every vehicle in the camp. It makes the machine heavier and noisier, and drivers complain they can hear themselves coming. The Speaker says a noisy vehicle is a vehicle that lives, and she has not yet been wrong about it.

### lb_up_ash_wraps
title: Ash Wraps
subtitle: Sail-cloth against the glow
unlock: research
field_note: RIG RADIO // WRAPS ON. KEEP THE HOOD UP. MA SAYS THE GLOW IS NOT A COLOUR.

Layers of sail-cloth stitched with lead thread and dipped in grey resin, worn over the chest and shoulders. Ash Wraps reduce the radiation that rises from the glowing drifts, so infantry can stay in the open longer and push further without falling back. They are heavy, hot and smell of resin. Every Veilwalker wears a set, and most Tinkers wear them under their coats.

### lb_up_quick_pack
title: Quick Pack
subtitle: Faster folding
unlock: research
field_note: CHALK BOARD // PACK IN SIXTEEN. PACK IN SIXTEEN OR SLEEP IN THE ASH.

A set of cable pulleys, spring latches and a foot pedal that lets a crew fold a rig in well under the usual time. Every crew agrees it saves lives during a raid. Before Quick Pack, a camp had to be packed in a quiet hour. After it, a camp can leave as soon as the first flare goes up over the ridge.

### lb_up_grapple
title: Grapple Hooks
subtitle: Heavier hooks, looser walls
unlock: research
field_note: RIG RADIO // HOOKS READY. HE WILL NOT KNOW WHAT HIT HIM. NEITHER WILL THE WALL.

Stronger hooks, a longer line and a steel spike on the end of each throw. Hook Runners with grapples take more from a silo before the alarm, and they can tear a gap through a wall for a few seconds before it knits back. The younger runners call Grapple Hooks the best thing the Bench has produced since the Patchwork.

### lb_up_salvage_rights
title: Salvage Rights
subtitle: Every wreck counts
unlock: research
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SALVAGE RIGHTS SIGNED. EVERY WRECK IS OURS. EVERY ONE.

A written claim, signed at the Gathering, that the caravan has first right to every wreck on the Drift. In practice it means better paperwork for the crews who find the wrecks, faster hauling with the Salvage Hauler, and more value for every scrap that comes home. Three other camps dispute the claim. The caravans have never bothered to answer them, because the wrecks keep coming home.

### lb_up_patchwork
title: Patchwork
subtitle: Repairs at a distance
unlock: research
field_note: RIG RADIO // PATCH KIT LIVE. TINKER ON THE HILL, TANK IN THE SAND. DON'T MOVE, WE ARE COMING.

A long-range repair kit rigged from a welder, a radio and a spare set of fittings, carried by Tinkers who can now mend vehicles from across a field. Patchwork lets a Tinker keep a convoy running under fire. It is the thing Tinkers most want to do and the thing they are most often told to stop. The result is a fighting rig that never quite matches the one it started as, which is the caravan's whole philosophy in one machine.
