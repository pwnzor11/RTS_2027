# The Left Behind

Status: approved by the owner 2026-11-04 (Q-38)

Entry ids use the prefix `faction_lb_` for faction pages and `lb_` for unit and structure pages. Format per entry:
`title:`, `subtitle:`, `unlock:`, optional `field_note:`, body. Leader names are invented. Nothing here contradicts
design 03 "Decided" or the canon in D-086 (Brigadier Wrenfield's brother was left behind on the Glass Coast in 1990).
Field notes read as caravan chalk-board messages and rig-radio chatter.

## Timeline 1990-2001 (for the Archive overview page)

- **Winter 1990.** The Great Drift buries the Glass Coast. The Authority orders an evacuation by convoy; the roads
  close under the ash before the last trains leave.
- **Spring 1990.** About four thousand people remain at the Tern Point railhead. The final convoy does not return.
- **1990-91.** The railhead families strip the stranded trains and build the first crawler-rigs.
- **1994.** The first caravan crosses the Drift to the dune sea. They are known to nobody and trade with no one.
- **1997.** Rig-masters hold the first Gathering at Kettle Flat. They choose a Speaker.
- **2001.** The Authority and Directorate meet openly. The caravans notice that both are looking at the Glass Coast.

### faction_lb_abandonment
title: The Last Train
subtitle: The abandonment, 1990
unlock: open

In the winter of 1990 the ash came down so thickly that it buried the coast road in a night. The Authority's convoy
masters called the evacuation; the trains were loaded in order of rank, then of clerkship, then of luck. When the
ash closed the road, there were four thousand people left at the Tern Point railhead with eleven working trucks and a
telegraph line that went dead on the third morning.

There was no letter, no official regret, and no search party. The people at Tern Point do not say they were
betrayed. They say they were counted, and that the count stopped. They kept the list they had made while they waited,
and every child of the camp is named on it.

### faction_lb_first_rigs
title: The First Rigs
subtitle: How the camp learned to move, 1990 to 1994
unlock: open

The stranded trains became the first homes. A boiler car was a kitchen; a flatbed was a market; a locomotive with its
reactor core gone cold was a workshop and a warning. By the second winter someone had bolted a cab and a pair of
tracks to a flatbed and driven it ten metres without it falling over. By the fourth, the whole camp could move.

The first caravan left Tern Point in 1994. The railhead was not abandoned a second time: the rigs were built so that
nothing, ever again, had to be left behind. Every rig carries a flag with the names of the people on it.

### faction_lb_culture
title: Caravan Custom
subtitle: How the Left Behind live
unlock: open

A rig is a family, a trade and a town. Rigs are named for what they carry or what they lost: *Second Chance*, *Kettle*,
*Nobody's Daughter*. Status is how long a rig has stayed afloat and how many strangers it has fed. Work is shared by
skill, not rank. Disputes are settled at the Gathering, which is held wherever the wind is kindest.

The caravans keep three habits with great discipline: they bury nobody they have not salvaged from, they never leave
a wreck without looking inside it, and they never say "goodbye" to someone leaving a rig, only "see you at the next
fire". Clothes are layered cloth over sheet metal; goggles are family heirlooms.

### faction_lb_leaders
title: The Speakers
subtitle: Leaders of the caravans
unlock: campaign

The caravans have no president. At each Gathering they choose a **Speaker**, who carries messages between rigs and
holds the ledger of debts. The present Speaker is **Marrow Tindall**, a former railhead telegraphist who still taps
her thumb in code when she thinks. She is patient, dry and exact, and she has not lost an argument she cared about.

Her war-captain is **Jessup Calloway-Ross**, the rig-master of *Kettle* and the best driver on the Drift. He is loud
where she is quiet, and the two have not agreed on anything in six years except that the Authority should be asked
first. The third voice is **Old Ottoline Vane**, the oldest woman on the Drift, who remembers the last train and has never
told anyone what she saw from the platform.

### faction_lb_authority
title: What the Left Behind Think of the Authority
subtitle: A long memory
unlock: campaign

The caravans do not hate the Authority; they simply remember the exact day the lists stopped. Some think the order to close the
road was a mistake. Others think it was arithmetic. Marrow Tindall holds that it makes no difference to the people on the
platform. When Authority survey cars appear on the dunes, the rigs offer water and a fair price for fuel, and do not offer a name.

An Authority officer, Brigadier Wrenfield, is known to the Drift. His brother was among those on the platform in 1990 and has
not been seen since; the caravans have a name for him, and the Brigadier has not yet asked for it.

### faction_lb_directorate
title: What the Left Behind Think of the Directorate
subtitle: Strangers with loudspeakers
unlock: campaign

The Directorate is a rumour that arrived as a voice. The first Directorate broadcast the caravans heard told them to
register for service. The caravans' reply was to retune the loudspeaker on the *Kettle* and play it back with a slightly
different wording. They do not fear the Voice, but they do not like what it does to the dunes at night.

### lb_scavenger
title: Scavenger
subtitle: Caravan rifleman
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SCAV CREW, FIVE ABOARD. BRING BACK THE PIPE, LEAVE THE BODY. WE KEEP LISTS.

Every child on the Drift is taught to pick through a wreck before they are taught to read. A Scavenger carries a scrap
rifle, a coil of rope and a spare filter tin. They are cheerful, grimy and very hard to surprise, and they treat every
fight as a chance to restock.

### lb_hook_runner
title: Hook Runner
subtitle: Raider on a line
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // HOOK ONE. IN AND OUT BEFORE THE ALARM FINISHES. DON'T TELL MA.

The best climbers on the Drift. A Hook Runner throws a weighted line over a wall or a silo rim, goes up, takes what
he can carry and drops back down before the first shot. The caravans call them "the quick hands", and every
rig keeps a bed for one.

### lb_slinger
title: Slinger
subtitle: Shaped-charge thrower
unlock: campaign
field_note: CHALK BOARD // SLING TEAM. TWO CHARGES EACH, EVERY CHARGE NAMED. WASTE ONE AND YOU PAINT THE NEXT.

Armed with a sling-arm and a bagful of hand-packed charges cast in tin cans, the Slinger answers armour the way the
caravans answer everything: with whatever is left over. They are brave in a quiet way and rarely stand still.

### lb_tinker
title: Tinker
subtitle: Field mechanic
unlock: open
field_note: RIG RADIO // TINKER ON DECK. KEEP HER ALIVE, SHE KEEPS EVERYTHING ELSE ALIVE.

Every rig has a Tinker, and every Tinker has a bag of odd tools. They patch plates, rewire valves, and bargain over the
price of a tube. The Left Behind will follow a Tinker into a fight they would otherwise avoid, because they know who
is going to fix them afterwards.

### lb_veilwalker
title: Veilwalker
subtitle: Dust scout
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // WALKER LOST IN THE GREY. SHE KNOWS WHERE SHE IS. WE DON'T. THAT IS THE POINT.

The Veilwalkers move in the ash, wrapped in sail-cloth the colour of the drifts. They are rarely seen, never
photographed, and are reported by the Authority as "environmental interference". The caravans simply call them the
quiet ones.

### lb_gleaner
title: Gleaner
subtitle: Ash harvester
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // GLEANER 3. HOPPER IS HALF FULL AND THE WIND IS TURNING. COME HOME.

A scrap-built scoop on a bus chassis. The Gleaner is slower, lighter and less respected than the Authority's Scraper,
and its drivers pay for it by being very good at running. A well-run caravan keeps three at the field and one at the
camp for spare parts.

### lb_harpoon_wagon
title: Harpoon Wagon
subtitle: Tethering anti-armour
unlock: campaign
field_note: CHALK BOARD // HARPOON TWO. HOLD HER STEADY. WE ARE NOT GOING TO PULL HIM OFF HIS TRACKS. WE ARE GOING TO TURN HIM.

A flatbed fitted with an old whaling gun and a drum of cable, bought from a coastal wreck and rebuilt a dozen times.
The harpoon hooks a hull and drags it off course. Enemy crews hate it most for the noise.

### lb_rolling_fort
title: Rolling Fort
subtitle: The caravans' heavy
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // FORT ON THE RIDGE. EVERYONE LOOK BUSY. LET THEM COME TO US.

Built from three wrecked buses, a bridge girder and an engine nobody can identify, the Fort is the biggest thing the
caravans can make that still moves. It is not fast and it is not pretty. It is, however, almost impossible to bring down
quickly, because every plate is a different age and every weak point is somewhere new.

### lb_mother_rig
title: Mother Rig
subtitle: The caravan's home and workshop
unlock: open
field_note: CHALK BOARD // MOTHER RIG UP. THE KETTLE IS ON. ALL RIGS REPORT.

The oldest rig in any caravan and the one that never gets rebuilt in the same shape twice. A Mother Rig packs down to a
single flatbed with four hundred names on its flag, and unpacks into a street. Everything that matters on the Drift
starts here, including the arguments.

### lb_second_drift
title: The Second Drift
subtitle: Superweapon
unlock: campaign
field_note: RIG RADIO // SPEAKER TO ALL RIGS. THE WIND IS OURS TODAY. KEEP YOUR MASKS ON.

The caravans do not have a bomb. What they have is thirty years of knowing how the ash moves. Using stolen anemometers,
a salvaged rocket pod and the memory of the winter that buried the coast, a rig-master can shape a storm that
buries a battlefield. It is the closest the Left Behind come to revenge, and the Speaker has never been seen to
smile when it is used.
